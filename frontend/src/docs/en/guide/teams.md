## Create the team

Open "Teams" in the sidebar, or under "More" on a phone, and choose "New team". Give it a name, say "Cash desk". "Description" and "Contact" are optional, and everyone who looks at the team sees them.

![The Teams page with waiting requests, the list of teams and the members of the chosen team](shot:teams)

## Decide how people join

The "Approval needed to join" switch is on: people ask, and you or a team lead decide. Turn it off and anyone in the event can join straight away.

## List the checks

If someone has to see something first, add it under "Checks before joining". Choose a template ("Extended police certificate", "First-aid course", "Driving licence class B") or "Something else".

For each check, set whether "Ask for the issue date" and "People may attach a file" apply, and under "Valid for (years)" how long it lasts. If it doesn't run out, leave that field empty.

Then choose "Save". With no checks, save straight away. A team with checks always needs approval.

## Reserve tasks for the team

Open a task and choose "Edit". Under "Who can book", choose "Only one team", pick your team under "Team" and choose "Save". Others still see the task, locked. Do this for each task that belongs to the team.

> **Good to know:** When you create a task, "Who can book" is in the "Event" step, once you've picked an existing event.

## Review requests

Waiting requests appear at the top of the Teams page, and you get an email and a push message. For a team without checks, choose "Approve" or "Decline" right there.

With checks, choose "Review request". Look at the document, tick "I've checked: …", enter the date under "Issued on" where asked, and choose, for example, "Approve Anna". "Decline" works without any ticks. Don't keep a copy: WirkSam only saves that you saw it, when, and the date. An attached file is deleted as soon as you decide.

![Reviewing a request to join, with a tick and the issue date for each check](shot:team-review)

> **Good to know:** You don't have to do this alone. On the Teams page, choose your team and then "Make team lead" in a member's menu. Team leads then review requests themselves.
