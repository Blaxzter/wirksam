title: Copy an event for next year
lede: You copy last year's event to new dates, choose which tasks come back and bring the same people across, while last year's sign-ups stay behind. You do this as an organiser of the old event, and the original stays exactly as it is. All you need is the new dates.
related: feature/shift-planning, guide/first-event, feature/members, guide/venue-map

## Start the copy

Open "Manage Events", which is under "More" on a phone. Every row has a copy icon, and past events are under "Expired events". For the event you currently have selected, there's also a "Copy this event" button at the top.

## Choose the new dates

Under "When it runs again", everything is filled in: the name ends in "(copy)", the start date is exactly 52 weeks later, so every task lands on the same day of the week, and the length stays the same.

Change the name and, if needed, the dates. The tasks move along by themselves.

## Choose the tasks and people

Under "What to bring across", all the tasks are ticked, up to 50 at a time. Untick whatever isn't happening again.

"Bring the same people across" is on. Organisers stay organisers, and people in charge come along if the people do. If you turn it off, you're alone in the new event.

Places, venue maps and teams always come across. Teams' ticked-off checks only hold for one event, though, so they have to be ticked again. The tasks' voucher settings come across, balances don't.

"What never comes across" names the rest: who signed up for which shift, who said they were free and when, open invitations and requests, reminders already sent and a place on the home screen.

## Check the shifts and status

Under "Check each task", open a task to adjust its name, place and dates. You decide two things there:

- **"Shifts for this task":** "Keep last time’s shifts" is selected, so every shift comes across as it was on the new dates, with its name and place. With "Plan the shifts again" you enter new times, shift length and headcount.
- **"Starts as":** "A draft" is selected. With "Open for booking", everyone in the new event can book as soon as the copy exists.

"Needs a look" on a task means something doesn't fit, usually the dates. The message inside the task says what it is.

## Tell people and create the copy

Under "Tell people", "Let everyone know" is off. If you turn it on, the people you bring across hear about the new event, with your own message if you like under "Add a few words".

"Look it over" shows the numbers and each task. "Create the copy" creates the event and opens its settings.

## Publish the tasks

Switch to the new event: tap the current event's name at the top of the sidebar, or on a phone "More" and "Change event". Under "Tasks", open each task that's still a draft and choose "Publish now". On a phone, tap the status next to the name instead. Until then, nobody can book its shifts.

The new event itself also starts as a draft. That keeps it hidden from outsiders, while the people in it can still see it.
