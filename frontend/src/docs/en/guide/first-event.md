## Create the event

After registering you land on "Select an event". On a phone, tap "Pick your event" first. Open the "My events" tab and choose "Create new event".

Enter a name, a start date and an end date. "Time zone" is filled in with your device's zone. Change it if the event takes place somewhere else, because shift times count in that zone. Under "Who can see this event", "Private" is selected, so only people you invite can find it. "Create event" takes you back to the list with your event already marked.

Choose "Continue", set how you'd like to be notified, and finish with "Finish setup".

## Add a task

Open "Tasks" and choose "Create Task". On smaller screens that's the round plus button at the bottom right.

The form has five sections, and "Next" takes you to the following one. Under "Task Details", a name is enough, say "Drinks stand". Under "Event", your event is already chosen. You can leave the rest as it is: by default, everyone in the event can book.

## Set the days and times

Under "Task Dates", choose "One day", "Date range" or "Specific dates", then the dates. They have to fall inside the event.

Under "Schedule & Shifts", set how a day is split up:

- "Default Start Time" [[start]] and "Default End Time", filled in with 10:00 and 18:00
- "Shift Duration" [[duration]], filled in with 30 minutes
- "People per Shift" [[people]], filled in with 2

If the time doesn't divide evenly, "Remaining time" asks what should happen to the rest. With several days, "Date Exceptions" lets you give one day different times.

![The "Schedule & Shifts" section of a new task, with the times, shift length and people per shift it starts with](shot:task-schedule)

## Check the preview and save

The "Preview" lists every shift you'll get, day by day. Tap a shift to leave it out, and tap again to bring it back.

When it looks right, choose "Create Task". You land on the new task's page. Add further tasks the same way.

## Publish the task

A new task is a draft, so volunteers can't see it yet. On its page, choose "Publish now". On a phone, tap the "Draft" status [[status]] next to the name and choose "Published".

Do this for each task.

![A task's page with the cards for people in charge and vouchers, and the shifts](shot:task-detail)

## Invite people

Open "Manage Events", which is under "More" on a phone. Tap the pencil in your event's row; on a phone, swipe the table sideways to reach it. On the "People" tab, further down, is the "Invitations" card:

- **By email:** enter addresses under "Email addresses" [[emails]] and choose "Send invitation".
- **By link:** "Create share link" [[link]] copies a link you can post in your group chat, for example.

Under "Join as" [[role]], "Member" is selected, which is all that booking needs. For people who should help plan, choose "Organiser".

It helps to send the people you invite the guide [Getting started as a volunteer](/guides/volunteer-start) as well.
