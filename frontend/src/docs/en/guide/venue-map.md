title: Create a venue map
lede: You create a venue map by uploading a plan or drawing your venue, adding a pin for each place people work, and saving the drawing as a template for next time. You do this as an organiser of the event, and it works on a phone too. To upload, you need a floor plan or a photo as PNG, JPG or WebP.

## Add your places

Open "Manage Events", which is under "More" on a phone, and tap the pencil for your event. On the "Locations" tab, use "Add location" for each place people work, such as the entrance, the kitchen or the main stage.

A name is enough. Volunteers see the description, address and contact later, when they open a shift.

## Create the map

Switch to the "Map" tab and choose "Add map". Give it a name, say "Grounds", and choose how to start:

- **"Draw it yourself":** under "Shape of the drawing area", pick landscape, portrait or square. This can't be changed later.
- **"Use a picture":** for your floor plan or photo. Export a PDF as an image first.

Confirm with "Create map". For a picture, you then choose the file with "Choose picture".

## Draw the venue

If the picture is enough, skip this step. Otherwise, drag out rooms with "Rectangle", and use "Shape" for free forms, "Line" for paths and "Text" for labels. "Decoration" adds trees, benches and gazebos. On a phone the tools sit below the map.

Everything saves by itself, and "Undo" takes back the last drawing step.

## Add the pins

"Add pins" lists all the places not on the map yet. On a phone, "Pins & picture" opens this list. Pick a place and tap the spot on the map. You can move a pin afterwards.

For toilets, first aid or parking, use "Other points". These symbols are only for finding your way.

Once the map has a picture or a drawing, everyone in the event finds it under "Map".

## Connect the shifts to the places

A pin's colour comes from the shifts at that place. When you create a task, choose the place from the list under "Location", and all its shifts take it on. The same goes for each new batch under "Add Shifts". A typed-in location doesn't count.

If you change a task's place later, its shifts follow, unless a shift has a place of its own. A batch with its own place is changed with "Edit" on that batch.

## Save it as a template

Open the ⋮ menu next to the maps and choose "Save as template…". Enter a name and confirm with "Save template". This keeps the drawing and the picture, without pins.

Only you can see the template. Next time, in any of your events, choose "Add map" and then "Start from a template".

If you copy the whole event you don't need a template, because its maps come across with their pins. See [Copy an event for next year](/guides/next-year).
