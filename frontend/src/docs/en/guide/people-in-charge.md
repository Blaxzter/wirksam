## Open the list of people in charge

Open the task. Its page has an "In charge" card, where you choose "Manage". The same thing is in the task's menu under "Manage people in charge".

![The task page with the cards for people in charge and vouchers](shot:task-detail)

> **Good to know:** You can also do this while creating a task. Pick an existing event in the "Event" step, and the "People in charge" section appears with the same field.

## Pick someone from the event

Under "Add a person", type a name or email address and pick the person from the list. It takes effect straight away, so there's nothing to save. They get an email and a push message with what they need to know.

## Invite anyone else by email address

If they aren't in the event yet, type their full email address and choose "Invite anna@example.com", or "Invite by e-mail" on a phone. They get an invitation and appear in the list as "Invited". They're in charge once they accept.

They can only accept with an account under exactly that address, so ask which one they use.

![The people in charge dialog with a typed address ready to be invited](shot:people-in-charge)

## Keep an eye on open invitations

An invitation is valid for two weeks. Each one shows in the list what you can do with it right now:

- **"Send again"** sends it once more, at most every ten minutes.
- **"Invite again"** appears once it has expired.
- **"Add now"** appears if the person has joined the event some other way.
- **"Withdraw invitation"** is for when it's no longer needed.

## Tell them what they can change

People in charge can change the task's name, description, place and category, add shifts, change the schedule and delete shifts. They see who booked, with contact details, and they see the task while it's a draft.

They can't publish or delete the task, decide who can book it, set up vouchers or name other people in charge. A booked shift that has started stays with you. The easiest way is to send them the page [People in charge](/features/people-in-charge).

If someone should no longer be in charge, open the menu on their row and choose "Remove". They can also choose "Step down" on the task page themselves, and you won't get a message about that.

> **Good to know:** On tasks that bring vouchers, people in charge can by default only change the task's details. You can allow more for the whole event, see [Vouchers](/features/vouchers).
