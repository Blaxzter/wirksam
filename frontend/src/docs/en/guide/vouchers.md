## Set the vouchers per shift

Open the task whose shifts should bring vouchers. Its page has a Vouchers card with a "Set up" button [[vouchers]]. Under "Vouchers per shift", enter how many each person gets per shift once it's over. 0 means none.

Do this for each task that should bring vouchers. Different numbers are fine, say two for the kitchen and three for the clear-down.

![The task page with the cards for people in charge and vouchers](shot:task-detail)

## Choose where vouchers are taken

In the same dialog, turn on "Vouchers are redeemed here" for the tasks that take vouchers, such as the kitchen or the drinks stand. The people in charge and everyone on shift there can then take vouchers in the app.

A task can do both: bring vouchers and take them.

## Check the rules

Once a task gives or takes vouchers, the event's settings have a Vouchers tab with two rules:

- **Redeem without a code:** [[no-code]] For people whose phone isn't working, you pick the person from a list. By default this also includes everyone on shift at a place that takes vouchers. If that's too open for you, switch to "Only organisers and the people in charge".
- **Shifts on tasks with vouchers:** [[shifts]] By default, only organisers add, move and delete them, because shifts decide how many vouchers someone gets. You can open this up to people in charge.

![The Vouchers tab with the two rules and the overview of who has how many vouchers](shot:vouchers-settings)

## Tell the stand

People at the stand open "Redeem vouchers", scan the code and choose the amount. It's easiest to send them the guide [Redeem vouchers at a stand](/guides/redeem-vouchers).

Volunteers find their vouchers under "My vouchers" and show their code there. They also see what's still to come from booked shifts.

> On tasks with vouchers, booking already ends when the shift starts. People who run the task can still book.

## Keep an eye on it and correct things

On the Vouchers tab, below the rules, is who has how many vouchers. With "Add correction" you add or take away vouchers, for example for help with the set-up that no shift covers. A correction always needs a reason.

If you later delete or move a shift that was already worked, its vouchers stay.
