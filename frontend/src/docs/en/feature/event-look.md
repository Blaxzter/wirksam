Someone who helps at the summer festival and at the Christmas market should see at a glance which of the two is open. So each event can have a logo, two colours and a base tone of its own.

![Try out colours on an example page](widget:event-look)

## Where you set it

As an organiser, open "Manage Events" and the pencil next to the event. On the "Details" tab, below the event's details, is the "Colour and logo" card.

There's nothing to save. A colour applies as soon as you pick it, and a logo as soon as it has uploaded.

![The "Colour and logo" card with a logo, its version for dark mode and the main colour](shot:event-appearance)

## The logo

"Upload logo" takes a PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF. A large picture is scaled down for you. "Replace logo" swaps it, and "Remove logo" takes it away again.

The logo sits straight on the page, so one with a see-through background looks best.

What a wide logo does is different from a square one:

- A wide logo, at least twice as wide as it is high, usually has the name in it already. It takes WirkSam's place at the top of the menu, on its own.
- A square logo sits there beside the event's name.

WirkSam is then only named at the foot of the menu.

The logo also appears on the home screen, when choosing an event, on the invitation page and on the printed roster. Where there's room for a small square only, as in the collapsed menu, a wide logo gives way to the event's first letter in its main colour.

## In dark mode

Most logos are drawn for a light page, and dark lettering would vanish on a dark one. So dark mode puts the logo on a white background to begin with.

Under "For dark mode" you can do better. "Upload dark version" takes a version for dark backgrounds, such as your logo with white lettering, and dark mode shows that one instead. With "White background in dark mode" switched off, the logo then sits straight on the dark page too. The box beside it shows how it looks in dark mode, whichever mode you're in.

The dark version belongs to the logo: removing the logo removes it too. The printed roster always shows the logo itself, since paper is white.

## Two colours and a base tone

"Main colour" is for buttons, links, the selected menu item and the large panels, such as your next shift on the home screen. You choose from red, orange, teal, blue, violet, pink and grey.

"Second colour" is for secondary buttons, selected rows and small labels, such as the countdown to your next shift. It also offers yellow and green, because it only ever sits under dark text.

"Base tone" is for the background, cards and menu, which are WirkSam's cream to begin with. "Like main colour" tints them in the main colour, "Neutral" makes them plain grey, and there are rose, green, teal, blue and violet too.

"Default" keeps WirkSam's own for each of them, so you can set just one.

Under "Own" you can pick any colour, such as your club's, with the colour field or as a code like #1e6bb8. WirkSam keeps its hue and makes it only as much lighter or darker as text needs, separately for light and dark mode. Below the picker it says what it did, for example "made darker so text stays readable: 4.7:1 instead of 2.1:1". For the base tone, only the hue of your colour is used.

Warnings and how well a shift is staffed keep their usual colours, so they mean the same in every event.

## Readable whatever you pick

Everyone still chooses light or dark mode for themselves. Each colour is tuned for both, so text on it stays easy to read with every main colour, second colour and base tone, your own included. Readable here means at least 4.5:1 contrast, what the accessibility guidelines (WCAG AA) ask of text. A base tone only changes the hue, never how light a surface is.

Without a base tone, dark mode still gives the background and the menu a hint of the main colour. Someone who picked the "Classic" palette keeps its black and white, with your main and second colour but no base tone.

## A description with formatting

The event's "Description" on the same tab can use **bold**, lists and links. People see it on their invitation, when choosing an event, on the event's details and on the printed roster.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who sees the logo and the colours?

Everyone in the event, while it's the event they have open. Someone who helps at two events sees each in its own look when they switch. People you invite see it already on the invitation page.

### Can I enter our exact brand colour?

Yes, under "Own". In light mode, a main colour dark enough to read as text stays exactly as you picked it. Otherwise WirkSam makes it a little lighter or darker, just enough for readable text, and tells you by how much.

### Does the look come along when we copy the event?

Yes. The logo with its dark version, both colours, the base tone and the white background setting all come across. The steps are in [Copy an event for next year](/guides/next-year).

### Can I try it in the demo?

The colours, yes. Logos can't be uploaded in a demo.

### Can people in charge or members change it?

No, only the event's organisers and its owner.
