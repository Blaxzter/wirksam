Whoever has run the kitchen for years shouldn't have to ask an organiser every time they want to add a shift or see who's coming on Saturday. A person in charge can do that without being able to run the whole event.

By default a task has none, and the organisers look after it. A task can have several people in charge, and one person can be in charge of several tasks.

## What people in charge can do

For their task, people in charge can:

- change its name, description, place and category
- add shifts, change the schedule and delete shifts
- see who booked, with their email address, phone number if they gave one, and any note on the booking
- see and edit the task while it's still a draft
- redeem vouchers, if the task takes them

Other volunteers only see names on a shift. Everyone in the event can see who's in charge, on the task page and in each shift's details, also by name only.

## What stays with the organisers

Only organisers can:

- publish, archive or delete a task
- reserve a task for a [team](/features/teams) or open it up again
- decide whether a task gives or takes vouchers
- move a task to another event
- name or invite people in charge

Shifts someone has already booked have a rule of their own: once such a shift has started, people in charge can't delete or move it, and a booked shift can't be moved into the past either. People's hours come from their booked shifts, so that's for the organisers to decide. Shifts with no bookings aren't affected. If changing the schedule would remove booked shifts, WirkSam shows who is affected first.

## Naming someone

The task page has an "In charge" card. Organisers open the list there with "Manage". Under "Add a person", search by name or email address and pick someone from the event. It takes effect straight away, so there's no button to save it. To take someone off, open the menu on their row and choose "Remove".

You can also do this while creating a task. Once you've picked an existing event in the "Event" step, the "People in charge" section appears. It's optional. If you're creating a new event in that step, name people on the task page afterwards.

## Inviting by email address

If someone isn't in the event yet, type their full email address into the same field and choose "Invite anna@example.com", or "Invite by e-mail" on a phone. They then appear in the list as "Invited" and are in charge once they accept.

If the address belongs to someone in the event, that person is in charge at once. Everyone else gets an invitation, whether or not they have an account, and you won't find out which.

Someone without an account gets an email in the language you use WirkSam in. It says:

- who is inviting them and which task it's about, with its place and dates
- what being in charge means and who else is in charge
- how to accept with "Create account and accept", or sign in with an existing account
- how long the invitation is valid, and that they needn't do anything if it's not for them

It never contains words of your own or the task's description. Someone with an account gets the invitation as a notification, by default by email and push and in their inbox.

The link opens a page with the event, the task and its dates. "Accept and join" makes them a member of the event and puts them in charge, and takes them straight to the task page, or to the home screen if there are several tasks. "Not now" changes nothing. They can only accept with an account under exactly that address.

If you invite the same address to another task, that task joins the open invitation, and one yes covers all of them. If the person joins the event some other way, such as through a share link, they don't become in charge by themselves. The list then says "Already in the event now", and "Add now" puts them in charge.

## Resending, withdrawing and limits

Only organisers see open invitations, on the card and in the list. An invitation is valid for two weeks.

- **"Send again"** sends it once more, at most every ten minutes, and keeps the link open for another two weeks.
- **"Invite again"** appears once it has expired.
- **"Withdraw invitation"** takes the task off the invitation. If it was only about this task, the link then says the invitation was withdrawn. If it came from the event's settings, the person can still join the event but won't be put in charge.

To stop anyone using WirkSam to send mail to strangers, there are limits:

- 200 new invitations per organiser in 24 hours
- 3 new invitations per address in 24 hours, across all events
- 20 addresses when creating a task; invite more from the task afterwards

A demo event can't invite anyone.

## Who gets told what

- **Named by someone else:** an email and a push message, plus an entry in the inbox. The email says who named them, the place and dates, how many shifts there are, who else is in charge and what they can now do.
- **Taken off by someone else:** a push message and an inbox entry. They only get an email if they turn it on.
- **Naming yourself or stepping down:** no message goes out. Organisers won't hear about it by themselves when someone steps down.
- **Invitation accepted:** the organisers get a push message and an inbox entry naming the task, whether or not the person was already in the event.

Everyone can change this under [notifications](/features/notifications), with "Put in charge of a task" and "No longer in charge".

## Keeping track and stepping down

Tasks you're in charge of come first under "Tasks" in the sidebar and carry a shield. They stay there until the task is over, even when every shift is taken. Next to the shield is the number of open shifts, or the word "Draft" for a draft. In the task list they're marked "In charge".

As a person in charge you see the drafts of your own tasks and no others. Their page says "Not visible to volunteers yet. The organisers can publish it."

When it no longer works for you, choose "Step down" on the "In charge" card. WirkSam first says who stays in charge, or that the organisers will look after it. Your own shifts stay booked. If the task isn't published, you won't see it any more afterwards.

## Tasks with vouchers

If a task brings vouchers, people in charge can by default only change its name, description, place and category. Adding shifts, changing the schedule and deleting shifts stay with the organisers, because every extra shift brings extra vouchers. A note on the "In charge" card says so.

If you trust them, turn it on for all tasks in the Vouchers tab of the event settings. Tasks without vouchers aren't affected. More under [Vouchers](/features/vouchers).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can people in charge name other people in charge?

No. Only organisers name, invite and take people off. People in charge can only step down themselves.

### What if the person's account uses a different address?

An invitation can only be accepted with an account under the exact address it went to. So invite the address they sign in with. If they're already in the event, you'll find them by name.

### Can people in charge book shifts reserved for a team?

Only if they're in the team themselves. Without being in the team, only organisers can.

### What happens when someone leaves the event?

Being in charge ends with it, for all tasks in that event. If they come back later, you have to name them again.

### Do people in charge come along when we copy the event for next year?

Only if you copy the members too. The guide [Copy an event for next year](/guides/next-year) shows how.
