Everyone books for themselves. Organisers decide what there is to book: tasks, shifts, how many people each shift needs and whether a task is reserved for a team.

It all runs in the browser, and installing the app is up to you.

## Finding a shift

There are three ways:

- **Home:** shows your next shift, your plan and, under "Still needs people", shifts with free places. "Sign up" opens the shift.
- **Tasks:** lists the event's tasks with a week strip of their shifts. There are also the "Cards" and "Calendar" views, a search and "Filters", such as "Hide fully booked" and "Only tasks I can book".
- **Map:** if the event has a [venue map](/features/venue-map), you can find a place's shifts there too.

![The task list with a week strip of shifts for each task](shot:tasks)

## How booking works

On a task's page, each shift is a tile with its time, how full it is and the first names of the people already on it. As a volunteer, tap a free tile, WirkSam asks "Do you want to book this shift?", and once you confirm it's booked. The "View details" icon on the tile shows you the place, the people in charge and who's on it first, and you can also sign up there with "Sign me up".

People who run the task, meaning organisers and people in charge, open the shift first when they tap and see who's on it. They carry on from there with "Sign me up". From the home screen and the week strip, the shift also opens first.

After that the booking is in "My Bookings", you get the message "Booking Confirmed", and your [reminders](/features/notifications) are set.

![A shift's details with its place, its people in charge and the people on it](shot:shift-dialog)

## What applies when booking

- **Members only:** you can book once you're part of the event. How to get there is under [Invitations and roles](/features/members).
- **Published tasks only:** only organisers and the task's people in charge see and book drafts.
- **Until the shift is over:** after that you can neither book nor cancel.
- **On tasks with [vouchers](/features/vouchers), only until it starts:** people who run the task can still book after that.
- **No overlaps:** you can't book a shift that overlaps one you've booked, even if the other one belongs to a different event. The tile shows this with a red icon. Back-to-back shifts are fine, and shifts without times never overlap.
- **Tasks for a [team](/features/teams):** approved team members and organisers can book them. The tile has a padlock, and when you open it you can join or ask to join.
- **Full shifts:** nothing happens when you tap a tile marked "Full".

## Cancelling

Tap your booked tile on the task's page, or open the booking and choose "Cancel Booking". After a confirmation the place is free again and its reminders are dropped. You can cancel until the shift ends, on tasks with vouchers too. As long as the rules above allow it, you can book the shift again later.

If the organisers delete a shift, your booking ends with it and WirkSam tells you. It then appears in "My Bookings" under "Cancelled", with the reason if one was given. Leaving an event cancels your bookings for shifts that aren't over yet.

## My Bookings

"My Bookings" ("Bookings" in the bar at the bottom of a phone) shows all your shifts in the selected event from today onwards. Above them are your hours and, if the event uses them, your vouchers. You can search, choose a different date range and arrange the list by date, task or place. "Cancelled" shows bookings that were cancelled (in the filter on a phone), and "Dismiss" takes them off your list.

A card opens the booking's page: date, time, place and how full it is, the way to the task, your reminders, who else is on it and "Cancel Booking". Under "My Notes" you can leave a note for the organisers, for example that you'll arrive late.

## Saying when you can help

Under "Availability" (under "More" on a phone) you tell the organisers when you can help. There are three ways to do it:

- **Fully available:** on all days of the event.
- **Daily window:** the same hours every day, and you can leave out single days.
- **Specific times:** you mark the hours for each day yourself.

On a phone they're called "Full", "Daily" and "Specific". "Save changes" at the top right enters it, and until then nothing is recorded. It applies to the selected event.

Organisers see under "Team availability" who can help at which hour. If they've set a "Default time window", your grid first shows only those hours, and you can show the others. This is a hint for planning: it doesn't book anything or block any shifts.

![The "My Availability" page with the three ways and the grid of days and hours](shot:availability)

## Shifts in your own calendar

In your profile menu, open "Account" and then "Calendar", and choose "Enable calendar sync". After that there are buttons for "Google Calendar", "Apple Calendar" and "Outlook", and "Add manually with URL" for anything else.

The calendar shows all your booked shifts from all your events, at the right moment even if you're somewhere else, because each entry carries its event's time zone. New ones appear and cancelled ones disappear the next time your calendar app checks. How often that is depends on the app. The entries carry your default reminders as alarms, or one hour before if you have none, and here too it's up to the app whether it shows them.

The link is personal: anyone who has it can see your shifts. "Regenerate URL" makes the old one stop working, and the switch pauses it all.

## Installing on a phone

You'll find the profile menu at the top right on a phone and at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer. It has "Install app". If your browser can do this itself, it opens its own prompt. On an iPhone or iPad, WirkSam explains the two steps: tap Share in Safari, then choose "Add to Home Screen".

WirkSam then opens like an app, without the browser around it. The entry is missing if your browser doesn't offer this or WirkSam is already installed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I book for someone else?

No. Everyone books with their own account. Organisers can't put anyone on a shift or take a single person off one either.

### Why can't I book a shift?

The tile usually says: it's full, over, or has already started on a task with vouchers, it overlaps one of your shifts, or it's reserved for a team. Open the shift to see the reason.

### Do the organisers find out when I cancel?

They don't get a message of their own, but your place is free again in the plan straight away. If it's at short notice, tell them yourself.

### Who can see what I've booked?

Everyone in the event who can see the task sees your name on the shift. Only organisers and the task's people in charge see your email address, phone number and note.

### Do I have to install the app?

No. Everything also works in the browser, on a phone and on a computer.
