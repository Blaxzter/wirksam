Everyone books for themselves. Organisers decide what there is to book. They set the tasks and shifts, how many people each shift needs and whether a task is reserved for a team.

You can book once you're part of the event. How to get there is under [Invitations and roles](/features/members). It all runs in the browser, and installing the app is up to you.

## Finding a shift

There are three places to find shifts:

- The home screen shows your next shift and your plan. Under "Still needs people" are shifts with free places, and "Sign up" opens the shift.
- The task list shows each task with a week strip of its shifts. There are also the "Cards" and "Calendar" views and a search. Under "Filters" you'll find options such as "Hide fully booked" and "Only tasks I can book".
- If the event has a [venue map](/features/venue-map), you can find a place's shifts there too.

![The task list with a week strip of shifts for each task](shot:tasks)

## How booking works

On a task's page, each shift is a tile. It shows the time, how full the shift is and the first names of the people already on it. As a volunteer, you book like this:

1. Tap a free tile.
2. WirkSam asks "Do you want to book this shift?"
3. Once you confirm, the shift is booked.

The "View details" icon on the tile shows you the place, the people in charge and who's on it first. You can also sign up there with "Sign me up".

Once you've booked, the shift is in "My Bookings". You get the message "Booking Confirmed", and your [reminders](/features/notifications) are set.

You can book and cancel until the shift is over.

> You can't book a shift that overlaps one you've booked. That applies even if the other one belongs to a different event.

![A shift's details with its place, its people in charge and the people on it](shot:shift-dialog)

## My Bookings and cancelling

"My Bookings" shows all your shifts in the selected event from today onwards. Above them are your hours and, if the event uses them, your vouchers. On a phone it's "Bookings" in the bar at the bottom.

You can search, choose a different date range and arrange the list by date, task or place. A card opens the booking's page, which shows:

- date, time, place and how full it is
- the way to the task
- your reminders
- who else is on it
- "Cancel Booking"

Under "My Notes" you can leave a note for the organisers, for example that you'll arrive late.

To cancel, choose "Cancel Booking" or tap your booked tile on the task's page. After a confirmation the place is free again and its reminders are dropped.

!["My Bookings" with the booked shifts as cards](shot:helper-bookings)

## Saying when you can help

Under "Availability" you tell the organisers when you can help. On a phone it's under "More". There are three ways to do it:

- **Fully available:** on all days of the event.
- **Daily window:** the same hours every day, and you can leave out single days.
- **Specific times:** you mark the hours for each day yourself.

On a phone they're called "Full", "Daily" and "Specific". Nothing is recorded until you press "Save changes" at the top right. It applies to the selected event.

Your availability is a hint for planning. It doesn't book anything or block any shifts.

![The "My Availability" page with the three ways and the grid of days and hours](shot:availability)

## Shifts in your own calendar

To get your shifts into your calendar:

1. In your profile menu, open "Account" and then "Calendar".
2. Choose "Enable calendar sync".
3. You now get buttons for "Google Calendar", "Apple Calendar" and "Outlook". For anything else there's "Add manually with URL".

The calendar shows all your booked shifts from all your events. Each entry carries its event's time zone, so the time is right even if you're somewhere else.

New shifts appear and cancelled ones disappear the next time your calendar app checks. How often that is depends on the app.

> The link is personal: anyone who has it can see your shifts. "Regenerate URL" makes the old one stop working, and the switch pauses it all.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I book for someone else?

No. Everyone books with their own account. Organisers can't put anyone on a shift or take a single person off one either.

### Why can't I book a shift?

The tile usually says. Open the shift to see the reason. Possible reasons:

- The tile is marked "Full". Then nothing happens when you tap it.
- The shift is over.
- The task uses [vouchers](/features/vouchers), and the shift has already started. There, booking ends when the shift starts, except for people who run the task.
- It overlaps one of your shifts.
- The task is reserved for a [team](/features/teams). Then only approved team members and organisers can book it. The tile has a padlock, and when you open it you can join or ask to join.

Only organisers and the task's people in charge see and book drafts.

### What counts as an overlap?

The tile shows an overlap with a red icon. Back-to-back shifts are fine. Shifts without times never overlap.

### Why does tapping a tile open the shift first?

If you run the task as an organiser or person in charge, a tap opens the shift first. You see who's on it and carry on from there with "Sign me up".

From the home screen and the week strip, the shift also opens first.

### Until when can I cancel?

Until the shift ends, on tasks with vouchers too. As long as the booking rules allow it, you can book the shift again later.

### Do the organisers find out when I cancel?

They don't get a message of their own, but your place is free again in the plan straight away. If it's at short notice, tell them yourself.

### Where do I see cancelled bookings?

In "My Bookings", "Cancelled" shows them (in the filter on a phone). "Dismiss" takes them off your list.

If the organisers delete a shift, your booking ends with it and WirkSam tells you. It then appears under "Cancelled", with the reason if one was given.

### What happens to my bookings if I leave the event?

Leaving cancels your bookings for shifts that aren't over yet.

### Who can see what I've booked?

Everyone in the event who can see the task sees your name on the shift. Only organisers and the task's people in charge see your email address, phone number and note.

### What do organisers see of my availability?

Under "Team availability" they see who can help at which hour. If they've set a "Default time window", your grid first shows only those hours. You can show the others.

### Does my calendar remind me of my shifts?

The entries carry your default reminders as alarms. If you have none, it's one hour before. Whether the calendar app shows the alarm is up to the app.

### Do I have to install the app?

No. Everything also works in the browser, on a phone and on a computer.

If you'd like to, you'll find "Install app" in the profile menu. That's at the top right on a phone and at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer. If your browser can do this itself, it opens its own prompt.

On an iPhone or iPad, WirkSam explains the two steps: tap Share in Safari, then choose "Add to Home Screen". WirkSam then opens like an app, without the browser around it.

The entry is missing if your browser doesn't offer this or WirkSam is already installed.
