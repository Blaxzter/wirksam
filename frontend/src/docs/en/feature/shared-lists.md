Not everyone who helps once a year wants an account. With a shared list you pick the shifts that still need people and get a link to post wherever those people are: the parents' group chat, the club newsletter, a poster with a QR code. Whoever opens it picks a shift, types their name and is signed up.

Members of the event keep booking with their account, as before. A list is an extra way in for everyone else, and it shows only the shifts on it.

## Sharing a list

Organisers, and people in charge of a task, find "Shared lists" in the sidebar, under "More" on a phone. "Share a list" leads through three steps:

1. **Tasks:** pick one or more tasks, then the days. "Leave out single shifts" drops single shifts too. Shifts that are added to these tasks later show up on the list as well.
2. **Settings:** the heading and a few words for whoever opens the link, what helpers have to give, what others see, how many shifts one person may take and how long the list stays open. "Where are you sharing the link?" is a note only you see. "Open the list" makes it live.
3. **Share:** the link [[link]], a QR code for a notice board [[qr]] with "Print notice" [[print]], and a text for your group [[text]] with the shifts that are free right now, which you can change before copying.

![The last step of "Share a list", with the link, the text for a group chat and the QR code](shot:shared-list-share)

The defaults:

- The email address is required, and the phone number isn't asked for.
- Others see only how many places are free.
- One person may take as many shifts as are free.
- The list stays open until its last shift starts.

Only published tasks of a published event can go on a list. Tasks reserved for a [team](/features/teams) can't, because only team members may book them.

## Printing a notice

A notice puts a list on paper: its heading and your words, its QR code with "Scan to sign up, no account needed", and the shifts it offers, by task and day, with the free places and the vouchers a shift gives. Each free place gets a line, so people can write their name on the paper as well. "Lines to sign up on paper" [[lines]] leaves those out, and "Names of those signed up" [[names]] fills in who is already down.

![A list's notice with its heading, the QR code and the shifts by day, with a line for each free place](shot:shared-list-notice)

"Print" on a task's page makes this notice for organisers and the people in charge of the task:

- If a list holds exactly that task, the notice is that list's. If the list is paused or expired, its notice says so and offers to resume or reopen it, rather than a second list for the same shifts. Only an ended list doesn't count.
- Otherwise it asks once and creates a list named after the task, with the defaults above. "Adjust first" opens the steps instead, with the task already picked.
- A task that can't go on a list (not published, only for a team, or no free place ahead) prints without a QR code, and the dialog says why.

Members print the task's sheet without a QR code, and nothing is created.

"Print notice" on a list's page opens its notice too. While a list is paused, expired or ended, the print options say so and offer to resume, reopen or share it again, so nobody hangs up a code that leads nowhere.

For the whole event, "Print all tasks" puts every task that can go on a list (published, open to everyone, with a shift still to come) onto one shared list, and each of their sheets shows its QR code. The dialog names the tasks that print without one. Printing again later uses the same list, even once some of its tasks are full or over.

## What helpers see

The link opens a page with the list's heading and your words, then the shifts by day, each with its time, place and free places. A full shift stays visible but can't be picked. On a longer list, helpers can narrow the shifts down to one task, one day or those with a place left.

After picking a shift, a helper enters their name and, as far as the list asks, an email address and phone number. With an address they can ask for a reminder by email the day before. The form tells them who will see what.

![A shared list as helpers see it, with the shifts by day and the free places](shot:shared-list-page)
![The form after picking a shift, with name and email address](shot:shared-list-signup)

With an email address they get a confirmation with a link to take their name off again. Without one, the page shows them that link to keep. On a shared device, such as a tablet at the stand, "Done – next person" empties the form, and after two minutes without a touch it empties itself.

A paused list says so, and an ended one says it's closed. Shifts that have started disappear from the list.

## Vouchers without an account

When a task gives [vouchers](/features/vouchers), its shifts on the list say how many. Whoever signs up through the list earns them just like a member, as soon as the shift is over.

They're redeemed with a code: helpers open the link from their confirmation email (or the one the page showed them), tap "Show code" and show it at the stand. It works for two minutes and only once, as for members. They can't be picked from the list for redeeming without a code, because only members are on it.

![The page behind the link from the confirmation email, with the vouchers, "Show code" and the shifts](shot:shared-list-manage)

## Confirming your address

Someone who signs up several times with the same email address, say on two lists, first gets a page of their own for each sign-up. The page behind the link in the email asks "Is this your address?". With "Yes, that's me", every sign-up confirmed with that address for the same event shows on one page, and its vouchers are added up. Each sign-up keeps its own name: when a family signs several people up with one address, each shift still shows the name given when signing up.

Only the link from an email can confirm, because anyone can type an address into the form. Opening the link alone confirms nothing.

Whoever has that link can change the sign-ups and redeem the vouchers. So the emails ask people not to forward them.

## How long the details are kept

The name, email address and phone number are deleted after the person's last shift, after 30 days on the hosted service. The page behind the link shows the exact date, and the form and the confirmation email give the period. Vouchers not redeemed by then expire. Shifts that were worked stay counted, without a name.

## Who sees what

- The members of the event see a helper's name at the shift, like any other booking, marked "no account".
- The email address and phone number go only to the organisers and the people in charge of that task.
- On the list itself, nobody's name appears unless you chose "First names as well". Then the first names of people who were told so when they signed up show at their shift. Members' names never appear there.

## Keeping an eye on your lists

The "Links" tab shows every list with its status (Open, Paused, Expired or Ended), how many signed up and how many places are free, and you can sort and filter it. A list's title, or "Details", opens its own page: who signed up through it, what it offers, its settings, and its link with "Copy", "QR code", "Print notice" and "Preview", with "Pause", "Resume", "Edit", "New link" and "End" under the title.

![A list's page, with who signed up through it and, beside that, its link and settings](shot:shared-list-detail)

- **Pause** stops sign-ups until you resume. The link stays the same.
- **New link** replaces the link. The old one stops working, and the sign-ups stay.
- **End** closes the list for good. Everyone signed up stays signed up.

"Pause all" stops every open list of the event at once, for example when a link has ended up somewhere it shouldn't be. The "Tasks" tab shows which tasks still have free places that are on no open list, and "What helpers see" shows the page as it looks right now.

## Removing sign-ups

On a list's page, organisers and people in charge can take people off a shift who signed up through that list: one at a time, several at once, or with "Remove this and all newer ones" after a wave of made-up sign-ups. "Let them know by email" is on by default. Leave it off for made-up sign-ups, so nobody is written to at an address that isn't theirs. Organisers can also delete the person's data at the same time, which helps when someone without an email address asks for it. Unredeemed vouchers then expire. If the person confirmed their address, every sign-up confirmed with it goes too, perhaps someone else's from the same family: the dialog says how many before you delete anything.

Shifts that are already over can't be removed.

## People in charge

[People in charge](/features/people-in-charge) can share lists for the tasks they look after. A list that also holds other tasks, they can only look at. Organisers can switch sharing off for them with "People in charge may share lists for their tasks". People in charge can then still pause and end their lists, but not open them again.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do helpers need an account?

No. A name is enough, plus an email address or phone number if the list asks for one. Anyone who helps more often can be invited to the event and book with a free account.

### Can one person sign up for several shifts?

Yes. After signing up, "Sign up for another shift" keeps their details. Under "Shifts per person" you can set a limit, which is counted by email address.

### How do helpers take their name off?

Through the link in their confirmation email, or the link the page showed them if they gave no address. That page also lets them delete their data, and "That wasn't me" undoes a sign-up someone else made with their address.

### Do I get vouchers without an account?

Yes. For a shift with vouchers you get them once it's over, also when you signed up through a list. You redeem them with the code you show on the page behind the link from your email.

### May I forward the confirmation email?

Better not. Whoever has the link in it can change your sign-ups and redeem your vouchers. Anyone helping with you is best off signing up themselves.

### What if the link ends up in the wrong hands?

Pause the list, or give it a new link: the old one stops working at once, and everyone who signed up stays signed up. "Pause all" stops every list of the event in one go.

### Do search engines find a list?

No. A list's page asks search engines to leave it out, and it isn't in the sitemap.
