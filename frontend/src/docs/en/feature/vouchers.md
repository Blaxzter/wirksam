Paper vouchers have to be printed, counted out, handed round and collected again. WirkSam already knows who worked which shift. So the balance follows by itself.

Vouchers only appear in an event once a task gives or takes them. Until then, everything looks the same as before.

## Earning vouchers

For each task, you set how many vouchers a shift brings. As soon as a booked shift is over, the vouchers are there. Nobody has to do anything for that.

What's already earned stays. That holds even if the shift is later deleted or moved, or you change the number per shift.

## What volunteers see

"My vouchers" shows what's available right now and what's still to come from booked shifts. It also shows what was earned, redeemed or corrected. The history is below that.

"Show my code" opens a QR code with six characters to type in. It works for two minutes and only once, then a new one appears on its own. The screen stays on while it's open.

## Redeeming at a stand

Any task can be a place that takes vouchers, say the kitchen or the drinks stand. Redeeming there goes like this:

1. Open "Redeem vouchers".
2. Scan the code with the camera or type it in.
3. Choose the amount. You can't redeem more than someone has.

These people can redeem:

- organisers
- the people in charge of that task
- everyone on shift there right now, counting 30 minutes before and after

![Redeeming vouchers, with the camera and the field to type a code](shot:vouchers-redeem)
![The list of people when redeeming without a code, with the number of vouchers next to each name](shot:vouchers-picker)

If someone has a flat battery, no signal or no smartphone, it works without a code too. You pick them from a list that shows how many vouchers they have. People with none left are greyed out.

> By default, everyone on shift at a place that takes vouchers can also redeem without a code. That applies to anyone who books a shift there.

## What organisers can set

Once an event uses vouchers, its settings have a Vouchers tab. It has two rules that apply to all the event's tasks:

- Who may redeem without a code. If the default is too open for you, switch to "Only organisers and the people in charge".
- Whether [people in charge](/features/people-in-charge) may add, move and delete shifts on tasks with vouchers. This is off by default, because every extra shift brings extra vouchers. People in charge can then only change the description and details there.

If you trust your people in charge, turn the second rule on for the whole event.

![The Vouchers tab with the two rules and the overview of who has how many vouchers](shot:vouchers-settings)

Below the rules is who has how many vouchers: earned, redeemed, available and still to come. There's a search and each person's history.

That's also where you enter corrections, for example for help with the set-up that no shift covers. A correction always needs a reason, so others can follow it later. It can't push a balance below zero.

## Booking rules

Because vouchers depend on bookings, a few rules apply to all tasks in WirkSam. You can only book:

- as a member of the event
- on published tasks
- until the shift is over

Once the shift is over, you can't cancel either. On tasks with vouchers, booking already ends when the shift starts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can we use paper vouchers and the app at the same time?

Yes. A task that doesn't take vouchers in the app can still take paper ones. WirkSam only counts what was redeemed in the app, though.

### What happens to the vouchers when a shift is deleted?

If the shift was already worked, the vouchers stay. For shifts that aren't over yet there's nothing to keep. Vouchers only appear when the shift ends.

### What happens if I change the number of vouchers per shift?

The new number only applies to shifts that aren't over yet. What's already earned stays.

### Can someone use the same code twice?

No. A code works once and for two minutes at most. If a bad connection sends a redemption twice, it only counts once.

### How do I make redeeming without a code stricter?

Switch to "Only organisers and the people in charge" in the Vouchers tab. Or let only a [team](/features/teams) book the shifts at the stand.

Without a code nobody has to show anything, which is why this has its own rule on purpose. The open default is handy at a busy counter.

### Until when can vouchers be redeemed?

In the app, until the event's end date. "My vouchers" stays reachable after that, so volunteers can still see their balance.

### Can anyone still book a shift with vouchers after it has started?

Yes, the people who run the task. For everyone else, booking ends when the shift starts.

### What happens to my bookings if I leave an event?

Your bookings for shifts that aren't over yet are cancelled.

### Does the rule for people in charge affect tasks without vouchers?

No. On tasks without vouchers, nothing changes for people in charge.

### Does this cost extra?

No. WirkSam is free, and vouchers are part of it.
