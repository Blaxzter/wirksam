Paper vouchers have to be printed, counted out, handed round and collected again. WirkSam already knows who worked which shift, so the balance follows by itself.

Vouchers stay out of the way until you use them. They only appear in an event once a task gives or takes them. Without that, everything looks the same as before.

## Earning vouchers

For each task, you set how many vouchers a shift brings. As soon as a booked shift is over they are there, without anyone doing anything.

Earned is earned. If the shift is later deleted or moved, or you change the number per shift, what was already earned stays. A new number only applies to shifts that aren't over yet.

## What volunteers see

"My vouchers" shows what's available right now, what was earned, redeemed or corrected, and what's still to come from booked shifts. The history is below that. The page stays reachable after the event.

"Show my code" opens a QR code with six characters to type in. It works for two minutes and only once, then a new one appears on its own. The screen stays on while it's open.

## Redeeming at a stand

Any task can be a place that takes vouchers, say the kitchen or the drinks stand. There you open "Redeem vouchers", scan the code with the camera or type it in, and choose the amount. You can't redeem more than someone has.

These people can redeem:

- organisers
- the people in charge of that task
- everyone on shift there right now, counting 30 minutes before and after

Redeeming in the app works until the event's end date. Volunteers can still see their balance after that.

## When a phone won't cooperate

Flat battery, no signal, no smartphone: you can then pick the person from a list that shows how many vouchers they have. People with none left are greyed out.

This has its own rule on purpose, because nobody has to show anything here. By default it also includes everyone on shift at a place that takes vouchers. That's handy at a busy counter, but it applies to anyone who books a shift there. If that's too open for you, switch to "Only organisers and the people in charge", or let only a [team](/features/teams) book the shifts at the stand.

## What organisers can set

Once an event uses vouchers, its settings have a Vouchers tab with two rules that apply to all its tasks: who may redeem without a code, and whether [people in charge](/features/people-in-charge) may add, move and delete shifts on tasks with vouchers.

The second rule is off by default, because every extra shift brings extra vouchers. People in charge can then change the description and details there. If you trust them, turn it on for the whole event. Tasks without vouchers aren't affected.

## Overview and corrections

Below the rules is who has how many vouchers: earned, redeemed, available and still to come, with a search and each person's history.

That's also where you enter corrections, for example for help with the set-up that no shift covers. A correction always needs a reason, so others can follow it later, and it can't push a balance below zero.

## Stricter booking rules

Because vouchers depend on bookings, a few rules apply to all tasks in WirkSam: you can only book as a member of the event, only on published tasks, and only until the shift is over. Cancelling also ends then.

On tasks with vouchers, booking already ends when the shift starts. People who run the task can still book. Leaving an event cancels your bookings for shifts that aren't over yet.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can we use paper vouchers and the app at the same time?

Yes. A task that doesn't take vouchers in the app can still take paper ones. WirkSam only counts what was redeemed in the app, though.

### What happens to the vouchers when a shift is deleted?

If the shift was already worked, the vouchers stay. For shifts that aren't over yet there's nothing to keep, because vouchers only appear when the shift ends.

### Can someone use the same code twice?

No. A code works once and for two minutes at most. If a bad connection sends a redemption twice, it only counts once.

### Does this cost extra?

No. WirkSam is free, and vouchers are simply part of it.
