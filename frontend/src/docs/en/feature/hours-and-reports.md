Hours are counted on their own, with nothing to turn on and no list to keep. Volunteers see their own hours on My Bookings, and organisers see everyone's in the event's settings.

The Reports page gives you an overview of all your events. You can also print every task and the whole event.

## What counts as worked

A shift counts once it's over and the person has a confirmed booking on it. Until then it's shown as planned and stays out of the total. Cancelled bookings don't count.

The length comes from the shift's start and end time.

> WirkSam doesn't record who actually turned up. A confirmed booking counts even if the person didn't come.

## What volunteers see

My Bookings has a "Your hours" card for the selected event. It shows:

- the hours worked
- what's still to come from booked shifts
- how the total splits into "From shifts" and "Additional"

On a phone it's a small "Hours" tile on the Bookings tab.

"Breakdown" lists every shift and all hours added by hand. Each entry shows who added it. Shifts still to come are marked "Upcoming".

## The organisers' overview

Under "Manage Events", open the event and choose the "People" tab. The "Worked hours" card there lists everyone who has hours or booked shifts, most hours first.

Each row shows shifts, shift hours, additional hours and the total. Click a row to see the shifts and entries behind it.

![The "Worked hours" card on the People tab with shifts, shift hours and the total for each person](shot:event-hours)

## Adding hours and exporting

"Add hours" is for work no shift covers: setting up the day before, an errand, or a shift someone covered without booking it.

You enter the volunteer, date, hours and "What was done". Hours are decimals, so 1.5 for an hour and a half, at most 24 per entry. The description is required.

Added hours count straight away and appear in the "Additional" column. "Export CSV" on the "Worked hours" card downloads the list, for example for certificates or thank-you letters.

## The Reports page

"Reports" is in the sidebar, and under "More" on a phone. You see the page once you organise at least one event.

It combines all the events you organise. There's no filter for a single one. It shows:

- "Total Bookings", split into confirmed and cancelled
- "Fill Rate": booked places against all places
- "Tasks", with the number of shifts and how many have at least one booking
- "Active Volunteers": everyone with a confirmed booking in the date range. "Total Volunteers" beside it also counts everyone else in those events
- "Bookings Over Time", by day or month
- "Bookings by Time of Day": confirmed bookings by their shift's start time, which shows your busy times
- "Most Active Volunteers": the ten people with the most confirmed bookings
- "By Category" and "Fill Rate by Task": places, bookings and fill rate in detail

![The Reports page with the numbers for bookings and fill rate and the bookings over time](shot:reports)

You choose the date range at the top. It goes by the shift's date, whenever it was booked.

"Export CSV" downloads all bookings in the range. On a phone it's in the menu with the three dots.

## Printing

On a task's page, the button with the printer opens the print view. Organisers find "Print" in the menu with the three dots. Anyone who can see the task can print it.

The sheet has one line for each place on a shift. At the top are the task, its days and a QR code with the note "Scan to sign up online". By default the lines stay empty, for names written in by hand.

Beside the sheet, "Display options" decide what goes on it. "Show booked names", for example, fills in who booked.

For the whole event, organisers find a printer icon at the top of the task list. "Print overview" puts every task on one sheet, with its days, place and category.

"Print all tasks" prints each task's sheet one after the other, with the same display options. With "Show booked names", that's a roster for the whole event.

## Frequently asked questions

### How are shifts past midnight or without times counted?

A shift past midnight comes out right: 22:00 to 02:00 is four hours. A shift without a start or end time counts as 0 hours. The "Worked hours" card says below the list how many of those there are.

### Someone booked but didn't come. Do the hours still count?

Yes. Once a shift has ended the booking can't be cancelled, and you can only add hours by hand. For a certificate, it's best to correct the number in the exported file.

### What happens to the hours when I move or delete a shift?

WirkSam works the hours out from the shift's times each time. If you change the times, the hours of everyone booked on it change too. If you move a worked shift into the future, it counts as planned again.

If you delete a shift, its hours are gone. [Vouchers](/features/vouchers) stay, though. If you still want to delete a worked shift, add the hours back afterwards with "Add hours".

### Can people in charge see other people's hours?

No. The overview and the export are for organisers only. People in charge see their own hours on My Bookings, like everyone else.

### What happens to the hours of people who leave the event?

They stay on the "Worked hours" card with the hours they worked. They're marked "no longer in the event" there. You can only add new hours for current members, though.

### Can I edit an entry or take hours away?

You can edit or delete an entry in the person's details. You can't take hours away with an entry.

### What's in the CSV file with the hours?

It includes every member of the event, also those with no hours, and every former member with hours. For each person it has the name, email, role, shifts worked, shift hours, additional hours, the total, and upcoming shifts and hours. The column headings are in English, and hours are decimals with a point, such as 1.50.

### Which list do I use for certificates?

The export from the "Worked hours" card. It includes the hours added by hand. The export from Reports lists single bookings, including cancelled ones and ones not worked yet.

### Why is my report empty?

It's usually the date range. By default it runs from the start of this month to the end of next month. If your event's shifts fall outside that, as with last year's event, set the range to its days.

### What's in the export from Reports?

Each booking in the date range gets its own line, including cancelled ones with their reason. Each line also has the name and email, shift, date, times, hours, place, category and task.

### Which display options are there for printing?

- "Show booked names" fills in who booked. Phone numbers only appear if you're an organiser or in charge of the task.
- "Show title column" and "Show location column" start out on when the shifts differ in title or place.
- "Repeat header on each page" is on by default.
- "Show location map" appears when the task has one place with a pin on the venue map.
- "Filter dates" appears when there are several days, and prints only the ones you choose.

### Where does the QR code on the printed overview lead?

Someone in the event gets to the event's tasks. Anyone else lands on the Discover tab, where they can ask to join a public event.
