Normally anyone in an event can book any shift. That doesn't suit every task. You want someone you know at the cash desk, and only people who have shown a police certificate looking after children.

If no task is reserved, booking works as before. Volunteers don't see the Teams menu item until there is a team.

## Reserving a task for a team

A team belongs to one event. A task can be reserved for one team, and a team can have many tasks.

When creating or editing a task, organisers set who can book its shifts under "Who can book". The default is "Everyone in the event". With "Only one team" you pick a team from the list, or create one there with "New team…".

Until you pick a team, everyone can still book. Organisers can always book these shifts, in the team or not. [People in charge](/features/people-in-charge) have to join the team first.

## What volunteers see

A reserved task stays visible to everyone in the event, but it's locked. For a team called Cash desk:

- The task list says "Cash desk only" with a padlock.
- The task page says "Only members of Cash desk can book these shifts."
- Each shift has a padlock.
- The way in is right there: "Join Cash desk" for an open team, "Ask to join Cash desk" if it needs approval. After asking, it says "Request sent".

On the Teams page, your own teams are under "Your teams" and the rest under "More teams". There you see what a team does and how many shifts are still open.

## Who gets into the team

New teams start with "Approval needed to join" on. Someone who wants in sends a request, with a note if they like. Then:

1. Team leads and organisers get an email and a push message.
2. The request appears at the top of the Teams page.
3. For a team without checks, they decide right in the row with "Approve" or "Decline".
4. The person hears the result by email and in their inbox. If they're in, a push message comes too.

A decline needs no reason. Someone who was declined can ask again.

Turn approval off and the team is open: anyone in the event can join straight away and book. That suits tasks where people can judge for themselves.

## Team leads

Organisers make a member a lead with "Make team lead" in the menu on their row. From their team under "Your teams", a team lead can:

- decide requests
- add people
- record checks
- remove members

Editing or deleting the team, reserving tasks, appointing leads and removing other leads stay with the organisers.

"Add someone from the event" puts a person straight into the team, with no request. They have to be in the event already. WirkSam tells them they're in, unless you added yourself.

## Asking for checks

A team can list up to ten checks that someone has to see before a person joins. A new team has none, and a team with checks always needs approval.

There are three templates, and you can change all of them:

- Extended police certificate: asks for the issue date, valid for 5 years, no file
- First-aid course: asks for the issue date, valid for 2 years, file allowed
- Driving licence class B: no date, doesn't run out, no file

"Something else" adds your own.

A request with checks opens with "Review request". Approving only works once every check is ticked. Where a check asks for the issue date, you enter that too.

If you add someone directly, the same ticks are needed as for a request.

![Reviewing a request to join, with a tick and the issue date for each check](shot:team-review)

## What stays after the check

A tick stores who checked, when, the issue date and until when the check is valid. WirkSam keeps no copy of the document. Team leads and organisers see in the member list which check is valid until when.

If "People may attach a file" is on for a check, the person may attach one if they like.

> WirkSam deletes an attached file as soon as the request is decided. After an approval, only the tick remains.

If a check runs out, nobody is locked out: the person stays in the team and can keep booking.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does WirkSam store the police certificate?

No. The "Extended police certificate" template takes no file: the person shows it, and you tick it off. WirkSam stores who saw it and when, the issue date and until when the check is valid.

That stays so unless you turn on "People may attach a file" for it yourself.

### How do I see only the tasks I can book?

Turn on "Only tasks I can book" in the filters. It's off by default.

### What happens to waiting requests when we turn approval off?

They are all approved when you save, and any files attached to them are deleted. The form says how many before you do.

### What can I set for a check?

You set what to check and can add a note. You decide whether to ask for the issue date and whether people may attach a file. And you set how many years the check is valid.

### Which issue dates does WirkSam accept?

None in the future, and none so old that the check would already have run out. Validity counts from the issue date. If a check doesn't ask for a date, it counts from the day of the check.

### Do I have to check someone again for each team?

You tick it yourself in each team. If another team of this event already checked them for a check with the same name, the review says so. The date is then already filled in.

This never carries over to another event.

### What happens to attached files?

The person can attach, replace or remove a file when asking, or later under "Your teams". It can be one PDF or photo (PNG, JPEG, WebP) per check, up to 5 MB. The person, the team leads and the organisers can open it, and other volunteers can't.

It is deleted as soon as the request is decided, whichever way. The same happens if the person withdraws the request or leaves the event.

When a photo is uploaded, WirkSam removes embedded data such as where it was taken. Demo events take no files.

### What happens when a check runs out?

The member list shows "Needs a new check" next to the person. Under "Your teams" they read: "One of your checks for this team has run out. You can still book; talk to the team lead about a new one." WirkSam sends no message about it.

Once you've seen a new one, choose "Record a new check" in the menu on their row and tick it.

The same flag appears if you add a check after someone joined. It also appears if you rename a check or start asking for its issue date. The old ticks then no longer apply.

### What happens when someone leaves the team?

Their booked shifts stay booked, and only new bookings stop. In a team with approval, the person has to ask again to come back. If they leave the whole event, they also lose their bookings for shifts that aren't over yet.

Their ticks go as soon as they leave the team or the event, or are removed.

### Can we reserve a task for a team later on?

Yes. Whoever has already booked stays booked. The rule only applies to new bookings.

### What happens if we delete a team?

Its tasks are open to everyone in the event again, and booked shifts stay booked. Its memberships and ticks go with it.

### Do teams and checks come along when we copy an event?

The teams and the checks they ask for do, the ticks don't. If you copy the members too, they stay in their teams. Where a team asks for checks, they show "Needs a new check".

Waiting requests stay behind. More in the guide [Copy an event for next year](/guides/next-year).
