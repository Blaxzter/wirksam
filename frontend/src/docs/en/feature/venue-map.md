New volunteers tend to ask the same things: where's stand 3, where are the toilets, where should I go? The map answers that. It also shows you where people are still missing.

## Upload a picture or draw it yourself

As an organiser you create maps in the event's settings, on the "Map" tab with "Add map". Each map gets a name, then you choose how to start:

- "Draw it yourself" is selected to begin with: an empty area in landscape, portrait or square. The shape can't be changed later.
- "Use a picture" takes a floor plan or a photo as PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 20 MB. Export a PDF as an image first.
- "Start from a template" begins with a drawing you saved earlier.

"Map" only appears in the navigation once a map has a picture or a drawing.

## Drawing the venue

For drawing there's "Rectangle", "Shape", "Line" and "Text". Under "Look" these become a stage, a building, a room, water or a walkway. "Decoration" adds trees, benches, gazebos and beer tables, plus hedges, fences, streams and sand.

"Other points" has symbols for "Entrance", "Info desk", "First aid", "Toilets", "Parking" and "Kitchen". You can place them as often as you like and give them a "Label", such as "North gate".

These symbols are only for finding your way, with no shifts and no colour. The legend says so too. The search still finds them.

## Pins for the places people work

You add a pin for each place people work:

1. Open "Add pins". It lists all the [event's places](/features/shift-planning) that aren't on this map yet.
2. Pick one.
3. Tap the spot on the map. Or use "Put it in the middle" and drag the pin to the right spot afterwards.

A place can have one pin per map. You still change its name and details on the "Locations" tab.

Instead of a pin, you can tie a rectangle or shape to a place under "Linked place". Tapping it then shows that place.

## Colour shows the staffing

Each place appears on the map as a label with a number, its name and the booked places, for example 3/8. The colour applies to the day shown:

- green, "Fully staffed": every place is booked
- orange, "Partly staffed": at least half are booked
- red, "Understaffed": fewer than half are booked
- grey, "No shifts": there are no shifts there that day

Change the day with the arrows. While the event is running the map starts on today, otherwise on its first day.

Tapping a place shows its description, address and contact plus that day's shifts. You can take one straight away with "Book".

The map also has:

- the "All places" list beside it, with the same numbers
- "My shifts", which highlights the places where you have a shift that day. It's on from the start.
- "Find a place", which searches across all maps

Volunteers also come across the map elsewhere:

- in an open shift under "Location and site plan"
- on a task's page, when all its shifts share one place
- if you like, on that task's printout

## Templates

If you run at the same venue every year, you don't have to draw it again. In a map's ⋮ menu, "Save as template…" keeps its drawing and picture, without pins. Areas lose their link to a place, because places belong to the event.

The template is yours: only you can see it. You can start from it in all your events with "Add map" and "Start from a template".

## On a phone

On a phone, the map is under "More". Two fingers zoom and one moves the map, and there are plus and minus buttons too. Tapping a place slides it up from the bottom with that day's shifts and "Book".

Where to find the rest:

- The search is behind the magnifying glass.
- The "All places" list and "My shifts" are in the ⋮ menu.
- The choice of maps is in the header.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I draw on a picture?

Yes, you can combine the two. You can draw on a picture, and "Put a picture underneath" adds one under a drawing later.

The drawing then stretches to the picture's proportions, so check that everything still sits right.

### Do I have to save the map?

No, everything saves by itself. "Undo" takes back drawing steps, but not pins you've placed.

### Do the pins stay when I replace the picture?

Yes, they stay where they are.

### Can an event have several maps?

Yes, say "Grounds" and "Hall, ground floor". They sit side by side as tabs. You change the order in the ⋮ menu with "Move to the left" and "Move to the right".

A place can have a pin on more than one map. Deleting a map removes its pins, and the places themselves stay.

### Why does a pin stay grey?

Grey means there are no shifts there on the day shown. Check the day, and check the shifts really have this place.

The counts only cover shifts linked to the place. A typed-in location doesn't count. It has to be chosen from the list of places. Volunteers see figures for published tasks, and for organisers drafts count too.

### Can volunteers book straight from the map?

Yes. A place shows that day's shifts with "Book". The same rules apply as anywhere else, for example when a shift is reserved for a [team](/features/teams).

### Who can see the map?

Everyone in the event, once a map has a picture or a drawing. Only organisers can change it, not people in charge. Nobody outside the event can see it.

### How many templates can I keep?

Up to 20. You can also rename or delete them in the list under "Start from a template". Later changes to the map don't change the template.

### Does the map come along when we copy the event?

Yes. Places, maps, pictures, drawings and pins always come across. The steps are in [Copy an event for next year](/guides/next-year).

### Can I edit the map on a phone?

Yes, as an organiser. The pencil at the top opens editing, and the tools then sit below the map. "Pins & picture" shows the list of places.

You place pins by tapping and move them with your finger.
