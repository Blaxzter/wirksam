A role always belongs to one event. Someone who organises one event can help at another as a member.

It all lives in the event's settings on the People tab: open requests, the list of everyone taking part, the hours and the invitations. Organisers get there through "Manage Events" and the pencil icon next to the event.

## The three roles

Whoever creates an event is its owner, and every event has exactly one. Anyone who joins later is a member by default.

- **Member:** sees the published tasks, books shifts, enters their own availability and sees their own hours.
- **Organiser:** creates and publishes tasks and shifts, names people in charge, sets up teams, the venue map and vouchers, and sees everyone's hours and the reports. On top of that come the people: inviting them, deciding requests, changing roles and removing someone.
- **Owner:** can do everything an organiser can, and is the only one who can delete the event or hand it over.

You change a role in the list of people, with the menu next to the name, and the person gets a message about it. Everyone in the event may see the list itself, with names and email addresses.

![The People tab with the list of everyone taking part and their roles](shot:event-people)

For a single task there are also [people in charge](/features/people-in-charge). That doesn't change anyone's role: a member looks after this one task and otherwise stays a member. You can also invite someone who isn't in the event yet directly, by email address.

## Inviting by email

Under "Invitations", enter one address or a whole list in "Email addresses", separated by commas, spaces or new lines. Up to 100 addresses go at once. Under "Join as" you choose the role, and "Member" is the default. "Send invitation" then sends it all off.

Someone with an account gets the invitation as an ordinary message, according to their own settings. Someone without one gets an email in the language of the person inviting them, with a button to create an account. It doesn't carry any text of your own.

This kind of invitation works for 14 days, once, and only for that address. Someone who signs in with a different address can't accept it. Open invitations are listed under "Waiting for a reply", where you can also withdraw them. One that has run out is marked "Expired": invite the address again, and withdraw the old one to clear the list. WirkSam skips addresses that are already taking part or already invited, and tells you which ones afterwards.

## Daily limits

So that nobody can use WirkSam to write to strangers, two limits apply to invitations by email:

- One person can send at most 200 invitations in 24 hours.
- One address gets at most 3 invitations in 24 hours, whichever event they come from.

Withdrawn and accepted invitations count too. Anything over the limit isn't sent. WirkSam tells you which addresses that affects, and you try again later. Links don't count.

## Inviting with a link

"Create share link" makes a link and copies it straight away. Share it wherever you like, for example in your club's group chat. Any number of people can use it, it works for 14 days and you don't need any addresses. Under "Share links" you see how often it was used, and you can withdraw it there. A link that has run out is marked "Expired" and can't be copied any more.

"Join as" applies here too. If you create the link with the role "Organiser", everyone who uses it becomes an organiser. For a big group, choose "Member".

Whoever opens an invitation sees the event, its dates, its description and the role, and decides with "Accept and join" or "Not now". Without a yes, nothing happens. What volunteers do at this point is in [Getting started as a volunteer](/guides/volunteer-start).

## Private or public

You choose this when creating the event, under "Who can see this event". You can change it later in the event's settings on the Details tab.

- **Private** (the default): only people you invite can find it or join it. For everyone else it doesn't exist.
- **Public:** anyone signed in can find it and ask to join.

A public event only shows to others once its status, on the same tab, is "Published". A draft or an archived event stays hidden from everyone who isn't in it. Publishing sends everyone in the event a message.

Public events are listed on the "Select an event" page on the Discover tab, with their name, dates, description and number of people. Tasks and shifts only show once you're a member. A new account without an event lands on this tab directly. At the top, "Recommended" shows events picked by whoever runs this WirkSam site.

## Deciding requests

For a public event, people tap "Ask to join" in Discover. Organisers get a message about it, their home screen shows a note, and the People tab shows the number of open requests.

Under "Requests to join" you decide with "Let in" or "Decline". Someone you let in becomes a member. The person hears the outcome either way, and someone who was declined can ask again. If they accept an invitation in the meantime, the request is settled by itself.

## Handing over an event

Only the owner sees "Hand over this event" on the People tab. Choose someone who is already taking part, in any role, and tap "Hand over". WirkSam asks you to confirm. They then take over at once and you stay on as an organiser, and only the new owner can hand it back.

## Leaving and removing

Next to your own name is "Leave". Organisers have an icon for removing someone next to other names. WirkSam asks you to confirm and says what will happen. Both do the same thing:

- Bookings for shifts that aren't over yet are cancelled. Shifts that are already over stay, because that work is done.
- Places in teams and being in charge of tasks go too. Someone who comes back later doesn't get these back automatically.
- WirkSam doesn't send the removed person a message, so tell them yourself.

The owner can't leave or be removed, and has to hand the event over first.

Members have no menu entry of their own for the People tab. It opens through "Open event" on a suitable message in the inbox. If there isn't one, the organisers can remove you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does everyone need an account?

Yes. Anyone who wants to take part creates an account when accepting, with a name, email address and password. It's free and works on a phone too.

### How long does an invitation last?

14 days, by email and by link alike. After that the person sees a note saying it has expired, and you send a new one.

### Someone didn't get the email. What now?

Withdraw the invitation under "Waiting for a reply" and send it again, or give the person a link. The second invitation counts towards the limit for that address.

### Can someone pass on our link?

There's no way to prevent that, because anyone with the link can join. Once you withdraw it, it stops working. People who have already joined stay in.

### Can we make a private event public later?

Yes. In the event's settings on the Details tab, choose "Public" under "Who can see this event" and save. It works the other way round too: a private event disappears from Discover, and people who are already in stay in. A copy for next year takes the setting from the original.
