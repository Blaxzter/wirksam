A role always belongs to one event. Someone who organises one event can help at another as a member.

It all lives in the event's settings on the People tab: open requests, everyone taking part, the hours and the invitations. Organisers get there through "Manage Events" and the pencil icon next to the event.

## The three roles

Whoever creates an event is its owner, and every event has exactly one. Anyone who joins later is a member by default.

- Members see the published tasks and book shifts. They enter their own availability and see their own hours.
- Organisers create and publish tasks and shifts. They name people in charge, set up teams, the venue map and vouchers, and see everyone's hours and the reports. They also invite people, decide requests, change roles and remove someone.
- Owners can do everything organisers can. Only they can delete the event or hand it over.

You change a role with the menu next to the name in the list of people. The person gets a message about it.

![The People tab with the list of everyone taking part and their roles](shot:event-people)

For a single task there are also [people in charge](/features/people-in-charge). That doesn't change anyone's role: a member looks after this one task and otherwise stays a member. You can invite someone who isn't in the event yet for this directly, by email address.

## Inviting by email

1. Under "Invitations", enter one address or a whole list in "Email addresses". Separate them with commas, spaces or new lines.
2. Choose the role under "Join as". "Member" is the default.
3. Tap "Send invitation".

The invitation works for 14 days and only for that one address. Open invitations are listed under "Waiting for a reply". You can withdraw them there too.

## Inviting with a link

"Create share link" makes a link and copies it straight away. Share it wherever you like, for example in your club's group chat.

Any number of people can use it, and you need no addresses. It works for 14 days. Under "Share links" you see how often it was used and can withdraw it.

> "Join as" applies here too. If you create the link with the role "Organiser", everyone who uses it becomes an organiser. For a big group, choose "Member".

## Private or public

You choose this when creating the event, under "Who can see this event". It's private by default, so only people you invite can find and join it. If it's public, anyone signed in can find it and ask to join.

Others only see a public event once its status on the Details tab is "Published". Publishing sends everyone in the event a message.

From then on the event is listed on the "Select an event" page on the Discover tab. Anyone who wants to take part taps "Ask to join" there. This is how organisers find out:

- They get a message.
- Their home screen shows a note.
- The People tab shows the number of open requests.

Under "Requests to join" you decide with "Let in" or "Decline". Someone you let in becomes a member. The person hears the outcome either way.

## Handing over, leaving, removing

Only the owner sees "Hand over this event" on the People tab.

1. Choose someone there who is already taking part. Their role doesn't matter.
2. Tap "Hand over" and confirm.

They take over at once and you stay on as an organiser. Only the new owner can hand it back.

Next to your own name is "Leave". Organisers have an icon for removing someone next to other names.

Either way, WirkSam asks you to confirm and says what will happen. Both do the same thing:

- Bookings for shifts that aren't over yet are cancelled. Shifts that are already over stay, because that work is done.
- Places in teams and being in charge of tasks go too. Someone who comes back later doesn't get these back automatically.
- WirkSam doesn't send the removed person a message, so tell them yourself.

The owner can't leave or be removed, and has to hand the event over first.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do people get when you invite them by email?

Someone with an account gets the invitation as an ordinary message, according to their own settings. Someone without one gets an email in the language of the person inviting them, with a button to create an account. It doesn't carry any text of your own.

### What happens when someone opens an invitation?

They see the event, its dates, its description and the role. They decide with "Accept and join" or "Not now", and without a yes nothing happens. What volunteers do at this point is in [Getting started as a volunteer](/guides/volunteer-start).

Everyone needs an account. Someone without one creates it when accepting, with a name, email address and password. It's free and works on a phone too.

### How long does an invitation last?

14 days, by email and by link alike. After that the person sees a note saying it has expired, and you send a new one.

In the list, an invitation that has run out is marked "Expired". Invite the address again and withdraw the old invitation to clear the list. A link that has run out is marked the same way and can't be copied any more.

### Can someone accept the invitation with a different address?

No. An invitation by email works once, and only for the address it went to. Someone who signs in with a different address can't accept it.

### What happens to addresses that are already in?

WirkSam skips addresses that are already taking part or already invited, and tells you which ones afterwards.

### Is there a limit on invitations?

Yes. Up to 100 addresses go at once. So that nobody can use WirkSam to write to strangers, two limits apply to invitations by email:

- One person can send at most 200 invitations in 24 hours.
- One address gets at most 3 invitations in 24 hours, whichever event they come from.

Withdrawn and accepted invitations count too, links don't. Anything over the limit isn't sent. WirkSam tells you which addresses that affects, and you try again later.

### Someone didn't get the email. What now?

Withdraw the invitation under "Waiting for a reply" and send it again, or give the person a link. The second invitation counts towards the limit for that address.

### Can someone pass on our link?

There's no way to prevent that, because anyone with the link can join. Once you withdraw it, it stops working. People who have already joined stay in.

### What does the Discover tab show?

Each public event is listed with its name, dates, description and number of people. Tasks and shifts only show once you're a member. At the top, "Recommended" shows events picked by whoever runs this WirkSam site.

A draft or an archived event stays hidden from everyone who isn't in it. A new account without an event lands on this tab directly.

### Can we make a private event public later?

Yes. In the event's settings on the Details tab, choose "Public" under "Who can see this event" and save.

It works the other way round too: a private event disappears from Discover, and people who are already in stay in. A copy for next year takes the setting from the original.

### Can someone ask again after being declined?

Yes, someone who was declined can ask again. If they accept an invitation while the request is open, the request is settled by itself.

### Who can see the list of people?

Everyone in the event, with names and email addresses. Members get there through their settings: under "Active Event" is the link "See who's in this event, or leave it".
