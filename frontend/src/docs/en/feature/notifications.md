Everyone decides for themselves what they get and how. Organisers don't set anything up for this, and they can't see or change anyone else's settings.

The defaults mean a new account gets the important things by email without doing anything. Push and Telegram need a quick yes or a connection once.

## The inbox

Every message about your shifts and events is also in the inbox, even if you've turned off every channel for that kind of message. On a phone it's in the bar at the bottom, and on a computer it's in the sidebar and behind the bell at the top right, each with the number of unread messages.

The newest are at the top, under "Today", "Yesterday" and "Earlier". At the top you can filter by "Reminders", "Updates", "Matches" and "Announcements". A card opens what it's about, such as the task, and then counts as read.

On a phone, swipe right to mark a message as read and left to delete it. "Mark all read" is next to the heading, and "Delete all" and the settings are in the menu beside it.

## The kinds of message

These are the kinds and their defaults. Telegram isn't on by default for any of them.

About your shifts:

- Booking Confirmed: email and push
- Booking Cancelled, when you cancel yourself: email
- Booking Cancelled by Organisers, when a shift you booked is removed: email and push
- Shift Co-booked, when someone else books your shift: push
- Shift Time Changed: email and push

About tasks and events:

- Task Published: email
- Event Published and Event Copied: email
- Event Invitation: email and push
- Role Changed: email and push
- Put in charge of a task: email and push
- No longer in charge: push
- Join Request Approved and Team Request Approved: email and push
- Join Request Declined and Team Request Declined: email

For organisers and team leads:

- Invitation Accepted: push
- Join Request and Team Request: email and push
- Shift Starting Soon (Unfilled), about half an hour before a shift that still has open places: email and push

There are also messages about your own account, for example if it's suspended.

## Email, push and Telegram

You'll find it all in your profile menu under "Notifications". The menu is at the top right on a phone and at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer. At the top of the page, "Delivery Channels" has one card for each channel.

- **Email** goes to your account's address and is on from the start.
- **Push** appears in the browser. Its switch is on from the start, but push can only arrive once you tap "Enable push notifications" and agree in the browser. "Send test notification" shows you whether it works. The card only appears if your browser can do push.
- **Telegram** is there if the WirkSam site you use has a Telegram bot. You connect your Telegram account on this card, then turn Telegram on for the kinds of message you want there.

Further down, "Per-notification Preferences" lists each kind with a switch per channel. WirkSam saves your changes by itself.

## Reminders before a shift

New accounts start with two default reminders: one day before by email and 15 minutes before by push. The message names the shift, date, time and place.

Reminders go by the event's time zone. If an organiser moves a shift or changes the zone, the scheduled reminders move with it, and ones already sent stay as they are. If the event isn't in the usual time zone of the WirkSam site you use, messages name its zone after the time.

You change that under "Default Booking Reminders". "Add reminder" offers times from 15 minutes to 2 days before the start, and for each one you choose the channels, with at least one staying on. Telegram is on offer once it's connected. This list is copied to every new booking. It doesn't change shifts you've already booked.

A channel you've switched off under "Delivery Channels" stays off for reminders too. They still land in your inbox.

For a single shift, open the booking. Its page has a "Reminders" card where you can set, change or remove up to five active reminders. Ones already sent stay there, greyed out. If you cancel the shift, its scheduled reminders are dropped.

## Which language

Messages come in German or English, in your account's language. At first that's the language you registered in. You can change it in your profile menu under "Account" and "Language", which also changes the language of the app. Email, push and Telegram follow it.

An invitation to an address without an account comes in the language of the person inviting. Task and shift names stay as the organisers wrote them.

## What you can turn off

- **One kind of message:** the switches in its row under "Per-notification Preferences".
- **One channel for all kinds:** the switch on its card under "Delivery Channels". This also stops your reminders before a shift on that channel.
- **Reminders:** under "Default Booking Reminders" for future bookings, and on the booking's page for shifts you've already booked.
- **Push and Telegram altogether:** "Disable" on the push card and "Disconnect" on the Telegram card.

A message still appears in the inbox. You can delete it there, one at a time or with "Delete all".

## Frequently asked questions

### Why don't push messages arrive?

Usually the browser hasn't been given permission. Tap "Enable push notifications" and try it with "Send test notification". If WirkSam says push is blocked, you need to allow it in your browser's settings.

### How do I turn reminders off completely?

Remove all of them under "Default Booking Reminders", and new bookings won't get any. For shifts you've already booked, remove them on the booking's page. The switches under "Delivery Channels" only stop them arriving on that channel, and they still land in your inbox.

### Do I get an email for every booking?

By default, yes: "Booking Confirmed" comes by email and push. If that's too much, turn it off in that row. Your reminders aren't affected.

### Do new default reminders also apply to shifts I've already booked?

No, only to new bookings. For shifts you've already booked, change them on the booking's page.

### Can I turn messages off for just one event?

The settings can't do that at the moment. They apply to all your events together.
