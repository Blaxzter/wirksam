Everyone decides for themselves what they get and how. Organisers don't set anything up for this. They also can't see or change anyone else's settings.

A new account gets the important things by email from the start, without you doing anything. Push and Telegram need a quick yes or a connection once.

## The inbox

Every message about your shifts and events is in the inbox. That's true even for kinds of message where you've turned off every channel.

On a phone the inbox is in the bar at the bottom. On a computer it's in the sidebar and behind the bell at the top right. Each place shows the number of unread messages.

- The newest are at the top, under "Today", "Yesterday" and "Earlier".
- At the top you can filter by "Reminders", "Updates", "Matches" and "Announcements".
- A card opens what it's about, such as the task. After that it counts as read.
- On a phone, swipe right to mark a message as read and left to delete it.

## Email, push and Telegram

You'll find your settings in the profile menu under "Notifications". The menu is at the top right on a phone and at the bottom of the sidebar on a computer.

At the top of the page, "Delivery Channels" has one card for each channel:

- Email goes to your account's address and is on from the start.
- Push appears in the browser. Its switch is on from the start. Push can only arrive, though, once you tap "Enable push notifications" and agree in the browser.
- Telegram needs your Telegram account, which you connect on its card. Then you turn Telegram on for the kinds of message you want there.

Further down, "Per-notification Preferences" lists each kind of message with a switch per channel. WirkSam saves your changes by itself.

![Notification settings with a row per kind of message and switches for email, push and Telegram](shot:notification-preferences)

## Reminders before a shift

New accounts start with two default reminders: one day before by email and 15 minutes before by push. The message names the shift, date, time and place.

You change that list under "Default Booking Reminders". "Add reminder" offers times from 15 minutes to 2 days before the start. For each one you choose the channels, and at least one stays on.

WirkSam copies the list to every new booking. For a single shift, open its booking. The booking's "Reminders" card lets you set, change or remove up to five active reminders.

## What you can turn off

- Turn off one kind of message with the switches in its row under "Per-notification Preferences".
- Turn off one channel for all kinds with the switch on its card under "Delivery Channels". Your reminders before a shift then stop using that channel too.
- Remove reminders for future bookings under "Default Booking Reminders". For shifts you've already booked, do it on the booking's page.
- Turn push off altogether with "Disable" on the push card, and Telegram with "Disconnect" on the Telegram card.

A message still appears in the inbox. You can delete it there, one at a time or with "Delete all".

## Frequently asked questions

### Which messages are there, and what are their defaults?

These are the kinds and their defaults. Telegram isn't on by default for any of them.

About your shifts:

- Booking Confirmed: email and push
- Booking Cancelled, when you cancel yourself: email
- Booking Cancelled by Organisers, when a shift you booked is removed: email and push
- Shift Co-booked, when someone else books your shift: push
- Shift Time Changed: email and push

About tasks and events:

- Task Published: email
- Event Published and Event Copied: email
- Event Invitation: email and push
- Role Changed: email and push
- Put in charge of a task: email and push
- No longer in charge: push
- Join Request Approved and Team Request Approved, which also covers being added to a team directly: email and push
- Join Request Declined and Team Request Declined: email

For organisers and team leads:

- Invitation Accepted: push
- Join Request and Team Request: email and push
- Shift Starting Soon (Unfilled), about half an hour before a shift that still has open places: push

There are also messages about your own account, for example if it's suspended.

### Why don't push messages arrive?

Usually the browser hasn't been given permission. Tap "Enable push notifications" and try it with "Send test notification". If WirkSam says push is blocked, you need to allow it in your browser's settings.

If there's no push card at all, your browser can't do push.

### Why don't I see Telegram?

Telegram is only there if the WirkSam site you use has a Telegram bot. For reminders, Telegram is on offer once you've connected it.

### Do I get an email for every booking?

By default, yes: "Booking Confirmed" comes by email and push. If that's too much, turn it off in that row. Your reminders aren't affected.

### Do new default reminders also apply to shifts I've already booked?

No, only to new bookings. For shifts you've already booked, change them on the booking's page. Reminders that were already sent stay there, greyed out.

### What happens to reminders if a shift is moved or I cancel?

Reminders go by the event's time zone. If an organiser moves a shift or changes the zone, the scheduled reminders move with it. Ones already sent stay as they are.

If you cancel the shift, its scheduled reminders are dropped.

### Why does a reminder show a time zone after the time?

The event isn't in the usual time zone of the WirkSam site you use. So the message names the event's zone as well.

### How do I turn reminders off completely?

Remove all of them under "Default Booking Reminders", and new bookings won't get any. For shifts you've already booked, remove them on the booking's page. The switches under "Delivery Channels" only stop them arriving on that channel, and they still land in your inbox.

### Can I mark all messages as read or delete them at once?

Yes. "Mark all read" is next to the inbox heading. "Delete all" and the settings are in the menu beside it.

### Which language do messages come in?

German or English, in your account's language. At first that's the language you registered in. Email, push and Telegram follow it.

You can change it in your profile menu under "Account" and "Language". That also changes the language of the app.

An invitation to an address without an account comes in the language of the person inviting. Task and shift names stay as the organisers wrote them.

### Can I turn messages off for just one event?

The settings can't do that at the moment. They apply to all your events together.
