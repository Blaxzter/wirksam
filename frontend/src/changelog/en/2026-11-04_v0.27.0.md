## A new home screen

The home screen now starts with the two questions you open it for: when am I on
next, and where are people still needed? At the top is your next shift in a large
green card, with the time, the place, a hint like "tomorrow" or "happening now"
and how many people are there with you. On a desktop, a bar shows where the shift
sits in the day.

Below that are "Your plan", with your shifts by day, and "Still needs people",
with open shifts. Organisers also see "Needs your attention": shifts that still
need people, open requests and drafts.

![The new home screen with the next shift, your vouchers, your plan and what needs the organisers' attention](./images/dashboard-organiser.png)

On a phone, the navigation at the bottom has a new Home tab at the front.

![The home screen on a phone, with tiles for vouchers and signed-up hours](./images/dashboard-volunteer.png)

## People in charge of a task

Until now, only organisers could change a task. With twenty stands, you either
made twenty people organisers or sent every small change through the organisers.
Now you can name people in charge of each task, on the task's page or right when
you create it.

People in charge can change their task's name, description, place and category,
look after its shifts, and see who booked and how to reach them. Publishing,
deleting, choosing the team and naming people in charge stay with the organisers.
So do booked shifts that have already started, because hours and vouchers depend
on them. Anyone who no longer wants the job can step down themselves.

![The task page with cards for the people in charge and vouchers, and the shifts below](./images/task-page-in-charge.png)

### Invite by e-mail address

The person doesn't have to be in the event yet. Enter their e-mail address in the
dialog and they get an invitation that names the task and explains what being in
charge means. When they accept, they join the event and are in charge. Until then
they are listed as "Invited" on the task, and you can send the invitation again or
withdraw it.

![The people in charge dialog with a new address to invite and one open invitation](./images/task-invite-dialog.png)

Being put in charge sends you an e-mail and a push notification; being taken off
sends a push notification. The sidebar marks your tasks with a shield.

## Vouchers

Many events thank their volunteers with vouchers for food and drinks, say two per
shift. Until now that meant paper, counted out by hand. WirkSam knows who worked
which shift, works out the balance and lets a stand take vouchers without paper.

Vouchers stay out of the way until you use them: they only appear in an event once
a task gives or takes them. Without that, everything looks the same as before.

### Earning vouchers

For each task, you set how many vouchers a shift brings. As soon as a booked shift
is over, the vouchers are there. And earned is earned: if the shift is later
deleted or moved, or you change the number per shift, what was already earned
stays. The only way to take it back is a correction, which can't push a balance
below zero.

"My vouchers" shows what's available, what was earned, redeemed or corrected, and
what's still to come from booked shifts, along with the history. The page stays
reachable after the event.

!["My vouchers" on a phone with five vouchers available and the history](./images/vouchers-my.png)

### Redeeming vouchers

"Show my code" opens a QR code with six characters to type in. It works for two
minutes and only once, a new one appears on its own, and the screen stays on while
it's open.

![The code to show, as a QR code and to type in](./images/vouchers-code.png)

At the stand, you open "Redeem vouchers", scan the code with the camera or type it
in, and choose the amount. Organisers, the stand's people in charge and everyone
on shift there can do this, counting 30 minutes before and after. Redeeming in the
app works until the event's end date.

If a phone won't cooperate, it also works without a code: you pick the person from
a list that shows how many vouchers they have. People with none left are greyed
out, and you can't redeem more than someone has.

![The list of people when redeeming without a code, with the number of vouchers next to each name](./images/vouchers-picker.png)

### What organisers can set

Once an event uses vouchers, its settings have a Vouchers tab with two rules:

- **Redeem without a code:** By default, this also includes everyone on shift at a
  place that takes vouchers. If that's too open for you, switch to "Only organisers
  and the people in charge", or let only a team book the shifts at the stand.
- **Shifts on tasks with vouchers:** By default, only organisers add, move and
  delete them, because shifts decide how many vouchers someone gets. People in
  charge can change the description and details there. If you trust them, turn it
  on for the whole event. Tasks without vouchers aren't affected.

Below that is who has how many vouchers, with a search and each person's history.
That's also where you enter corrections, always with a reason.

![The Vouchers tab with the two rules and the overview of who has how many vouchers](./images/vouchers-settings.png)

### Stricter booking rules

Because vouchers depend on bookings, a few new rules apply to all tasks. You can
only book as a member of the event, only on published tasks, and only until the
shift is over. Cancelling also ends then. On tasks with vouchers, booking already
ends when the shift starts. People who run a task can still book its drafts and
shifts that have started. Leaving an event cancels your bookings for shifts that
aren't over yet.

## The task page, rearranged

The task page now starts with two cards: who's in charge, and what there is in
vouchers and how many have been redeemed here. On a phone, actions slide up from the
bottom.

Shifts are tiles with the time, a bar and what's still free. They now also say
who's on the shift, for example "3 of 4 · Lena, Sofia, David". Longer lists end in
"+2". Anyone who can open the task sees the names, as they already could in the
shift dialog.

The shift dialog shows the place, the vouchers, who's in charge and who's on the
shift at a glance.

![The shift dialog with the place, vouchers, people in charge and the four people on the shift](./images/shift-dialog-roster.png)

The task list changed too: its card now counts the whole task and offers a jump to
the next shift. Fully booked tasks no longer disappear for the people who run them.

## My Bookings and the inbox

"My Bookings" shows hours and vouchers side by side, with your shifts below as
cards you can open. Search and filters are now easy to use on a phone too.

The inbox shows messages as cards, sorted under Today, Yesterday and Earlier.

!["My Bookings" on a phone, with tiles for vouchers and hours and the booked shifts](./images/bookings-cards.png)

![The inbox on a phone, with messages as cards under "Today"](./images/inbox-cards.png)

## E-mails that look like the app

Every e-mail from WirkSam now looks like the app, and the "[WirkSam]" at the start
of the subject is gone. Invitations say who's inviting you and, when a task is
involved, what it's called, where and when it takes place, and who's already on
board.

A bug went with it: invitations to addresses without an account never sent an
e-mail. Now they do. To keep them from being used for spam, there are limits per
day: 200 invitations per person and 3 to the same address. Sending one again works
once every 10 minutes.

![An invitation e-mail: Lena Hartmann asks you to take on "Kitchen & Café"](./images/mail-invitation.png)

## Try it in the demo

The demo event now has people in charge, vouchers and places to redeem them. You
can try all of it without setting anything up.

## Smaller improvements

- On a task with several series of shifts, you can edit the name, description,
  place and category again. That stopped working as soon as a second series was
  added.
- When you rename a task, the shift titles and series named after it follow.
- The "Hide fully booked" filter no longer sticks.
- Shifts that are over no longer ask for people in orange.
- The collapsed sidebar shows only icons again, and actions that appear on hover
  are back. "Tasks" and "My Bookings" now also show their names on hover there.
- The close buttons in dialogs are easier to hit.
- When you close a dialog, keyboard focus goes back to the button that opened it.
- Availability only takes input once your saved answers have loaded.
- The inbox follows your 12 or 24 hour setting.
- The old "Task Manager" switch in user management is gone. It hadn't done anything
  for a while.
