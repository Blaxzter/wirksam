## The top bar stays at the top

The bar with the bell and the breadcrumbs no longer scrolls away. However far down a
page you are, your notifications and the way back are one click away.

## Tabs and filters come along

On long pages, the row of controls under the heading now stops under the top bar instead
of scrolling out of sight:

- the tabs in an event's settings
- search and filters on Tasks and My Bookings
- the categories in your inbox
- on a phone, the section buttons in Settings and in What's New

Platform administrators get the same in Manage Events and User Management.
