## Numbers that show where to look

The guides now point at the screen they describe. Where a step names a field, a number
in the text matches a number on the screenshot. Point at either one and that part of the
screen is outlined while the rest dims, in the enlarged picture too.

![A step of the first-event guide: "Shift Duration" has the number 2 in the text and on the screenshot, where it is outlined](./images/guide-marks.png)

## On a computer or on a phone

Every screenshot on the feature pages and guides is now taken twice, on a computer and on
a phone. A switch beside the contents picks which one you see. It starts with the device
you're reading on and remembers your choice.

## A new look for the pages about WirkSam

Each feature page and guide has a drawn header beside its title, a colour for its group
and an "In short" box with three points. The texts are shorter and plainer. On the start
page, each row shows the part of the app it talks about and links to its page, and the
"Who it's for" section is gone.

![The top of the "Shift planning" page with its drawn header and the three points in short](./images/page-header.png)

## Fixed

- If a task's shifts have a length the list doesn't offer, "Add shifts" showed an empty
  "Shift duration". It now shows the length the task uses. The list also offers 3 and 4
  hours now.
- On a device set to a 12-hour clock, "Add shifts" filled in times like "07:00 PM", which
  were cut off and read as the morning. It now fills in the task's times as they are.
