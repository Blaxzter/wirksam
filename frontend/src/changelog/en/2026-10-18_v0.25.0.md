## WirkSam Compared

A new page sets WirkSam next to Engelsystem, Helferstube, helferplanung.app,
Doodle and a shared spreadsheet: what each one costs, whether helpers need an
account, whether it is open source and can run on your own server, which
languages it speaks and how it reminds people of their shifts. Every tool,
WirkSam included, says when it is a good fit and when another one serves you
better, with links to where the facts come from. You find it in the footer.

## Planning a Club Festival

The first page for a specific kind of event walks through planning the helpers
for a club festival, summer party or sports day: setting up each stand as a
job, letting the shifts be generated, inviting people with a link, and what
happens on the day. It ends with answers to the questions that come up most.

## Faster to Load

The public pages are now sent compressed and the browser keeps the app's files
instead of fetching them again on every visit, so the first page appears much
sooner, above all on a phone.

## Terms and Privacy

- The terms of use now say that we may limit the hosted service, for example
  by pausing new sign-ups, or shut it down. A shutdown is announced by email,
  usually at least four weeks ahead, and the privacy policy says that the data
  is deleted after that date. The code stays open, so you can always run
  WirkSam yourself.
- Changes to the terms are now announced by email four weeks before they take
  effect, and you can object or delete your account before then.
- WirkSam is described as what it is for: volunteers at events, in clubs,
  congregations and other groups, no longer church events only.
