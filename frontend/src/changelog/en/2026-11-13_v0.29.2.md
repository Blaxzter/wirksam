version: 0.29.2
date: 2026-11-13T16:30:00

## Tabs switch in place

In an event's settings, switching tabs no longer reloads the page. The heading and the
tab row stay where they are, the Map tab included.

Teams are no longer in that tab row. They have a page of their own, which you reach from
the menu.

## The Teams page loads in one go

The waiting team requests now appear together with the teams, instead of a moment later
pushing everything down.
