## Your logo and colours

An event can now look like the club behind it. Organisers give it a logo, a main colour, a second colour and a base tone, and while the event is open, everyone in it sees the app in that look: the logo at the top of the menu, and buttons, panels, cards and the menu in its colours.

![The dashboard of an event with its own look: the club's logo at the top of the menu and beside the greeting, a blue panel for the next shift and a yellow countdown](./images/event-look.png)

You'll find it under Event → Details → "Colour and logo". Out of the box, an event looks as before; nothing changes until an organiser picks something.

- **Logo.** A wide logo with the name in it takes WirkSam's place at the top of the menu; a square one sits beside the event's name. It also shows on the dashboard, in the event switcher, on the invitation page and on the printed roster. For dark mode you can add a version of its own, for example with white lettering, and decide whether the logo gets a white background there.
- **Main colour and second colour.** The main colour is for buttons, links, the selected menu item and the large panels, the second for smaller highlights such as the countdown to your next shift. Pick one of the presets, or "Own" with a colour field or a code like `#1e6bb8`.
- **Base tone.** The tint of the background, the cards and the menu, so the whole page leans towards your colour. It changes only the tint, never how light the page is.
- **Readable, whatever you pick.** Every colour is checked against the page in light and dark mode. If text on an own colour would be hard to read, the colour is made darker or lighter, keeping its hue, and the picker says what it did and how well the text reads now.

![The "Colour and logo" card: the logo, its dark-mode version and the colour presets with "Own" at the end](./images/event-appearance.png)

Invited people see the look on the invitation before they join, and a copied event keeps it. Warnings keep their own colours, so a full or empty shift looks the same in every event.

On the website, the new page [Logo and colours](/features/event-look) lets you try the colours on an example page, in light and dark mode, before you change anything in your event.

## Also new

- An event's description can have bold text, lists and links, as a location's already could.
- The printed overview of an event shows its logo and the formatted description.
