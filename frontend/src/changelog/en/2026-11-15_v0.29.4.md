## Rows that scroll sideways look tidier

When a row of tabs or filters is wider than the screen, the browser's own scrollbar no
longer sits underneath it. Instead the edge where more is hidden fades out. With a
mouse, a thin bar appears under the row while you point at it.

You'll see this in an event's settings, the categories in your inbox, the month picker
in the calendar, the team filters, My Availability and the decoration strip of the map
editor.

## Choosing from a list on a phone

On a phone, a list to choose from now opens in the middle of the screen instead of as a
small menu beside the field: bigger rows, your current choice ticked and in view, and the
field's name on top.

The last lists that still used the browser's own picker now look like all the others: a
member's role and the new owner on the People tab, the role in an invitation, the
volunteer when you add hours, the place when you redeem vouchers, and a pin's kind and a
shape's place in the map editor.
