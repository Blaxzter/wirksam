## The Demo Tour, One Thing at a Time

The guided tour in the demo now points at one thing per step and explains just
that, instead of describing a whole screen at once. As a helper you're walked
through your next shifts, searching and filtering the jobs, what a single row
and a single shift show, where to go and who's already on a shift, your hours,
and how to tell organisers when you have time. As an organiser you go through
the job form field by field, then the join request, the member list, worked
hours, invitations and each part of the reports. Each step is short, so the
tour has more of them, and you can still close it at any point and restart it
from the demo banner.
