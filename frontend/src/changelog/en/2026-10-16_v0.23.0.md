## A Map of Your Venue

Every event now has a **Map**. Upload a floor plan or a site picture, or draw
the venue yourself right in the app: rooms, paths, labels and areas, finished
off with trees, hedges, benches, tents and more. Then put a pin on it for each
place where people work. Everyone sees at a glance how well each place is
staffed on the chosen day, can tap a place to see its shifts, and can search
for where they need to be. Toilets, first aid and the info desk get their own
symbols, so nobody has to ask the way.

The map works on phones too, both for looking and for editing. Selected
decorations can be turned and resized right on the map with their grips.

## Places Instead of Free Text

A task's location is now a real place of the event instead of a line of text.
Places are picked from a list, so "Main hall" and "main hall " no longer end up
as two different places, and a place you rename is renamed everywhere at once.

## Reuse a Map You've Drawn

Drawn the same venue last year? Save a map's drawing as a **template**, and
start the next event's map from it. Only the drawing is copied, so you then
place the pins for the new event. Your templates are yours alone.

## No More Being in Two Places at Once

WirkSam no longer lets you book a shift that overlaps one you already have, in
any event. The message tells you which shift it clashes with, and clashing
shifts are marked before you even try. Back-to-back shifts are still fine.

## Hours Worked

Hours are now counted for every volunteer, from the shifts they actually
worked. You see your own total on **My Bookings**. Event admins find everyone's
hours in the event settings, can add hours for work no shift describes, and can
export the list as CSV.

## Small Things

- Privacy, terms and the legal notice have moved from under every page into the
  menu behind your name.
- Buttons now explain themselves with the same tooltips everywhere.
