## A time zone for each event

Each event now has its own time zone, which you set when you create it or in its
settings. Shift times count in that zone, so 10:00 means 10:00 at the venue, wherever
someone opens the plan. Reminders, calendars and vouchers all go by it, as does the
question of whether a shift has started. Existing events keep the time they've been
using.

If you change the zone later, a shift at 10:00 stays at 10:00 and reminders move with
it. Anyone whose device is set to another zone sees a short note on the task and on
their booking. If everyone is in the same zone, nothing looks different.

Shifts in your calendar now carry their time zone too, and a shift that runs past
midnight ends the next morning instead of before it starts.

## Publish an event and choose who sees it

An event's settings now let you publish it and change who can see it. Until now, a
copied event stayed a draft for good, and the choice between public and private was
final.

## Pages about WirkSam, and short guides

The public site now has a page for each part of WirkSam, with screenshots and answers
to common questions. Short guides walk you through things like your first event,
your first shift as a volunteer or copying an event for next year.

## Messages

- Organisers get a push notification about half an hour before a shift that still has
  open places. E-mail for this can be switched on in the settings.
- If a shift you booked is removed, you're now told. Shifts that are already over
  send nothing.
- Someone added to a team directly now gets a message.
- Organisers now hear when a member accepts tasks through an invitation.
- A channel you switch off now stays off, reminders included. Reminders still land in
  your inbox.
- The setting "Availability Reminder" is gone. Nothing ever sent it.

## Asking first

- Leaving an event, removing someone and handing an event over now ask first and say
  what will happen.
- Switching off approval for a team warns you when that would let people in who are
  still waiting.
- Expired invitations and share links are now marked as expired.

## Also new

- **Printing:** Organisers can now print the whole event, through the printer icon in
  the task list. Unticking names now takes them off the sheet, and the QR code on a
  sheet now opens the event.
- **Places:** If you change a task's place, its shifts follow, unless a shift has a
  place of its own. Regenerating shifts now keeps what you typed in by hand.
- **Members:** Members now read an event's details instead of seeing a form they can't
  save. Their settings have a new link to who's in the event, where they can also leave it.
- **Reports:** The number of volunteers now counts only people in the events the
  report covers, and the date range no longer ends a day early.
- **Vouchers:** An archived task no longer takes vouchers.
- **Venue map:** On a phone, "Use a picture" now opens right where you choose the file.
- **New events:** A new or copied event now opens with all its tabs, without reloading.
- **Far from the venue:** The buttons on shifts now go by the event's clock, so they
  match what's actually allowed.
- **Hosting WirkSam yourself:** An unchanged `.env.example` no longer stops the backend.
