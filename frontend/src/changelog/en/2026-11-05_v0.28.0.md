## A time zone for each event

Each event now has its own time zone, which you set when you create it or in its
settings. Shift times count in that zone, so 10:00 means 10:00 at the venue, wherever
someone opens the plan. Reminders, calendars and vouchers all go by it, as does the
question of whether a shift has started. Existing events keep the time they've been
using.

If you change the zone later, a shift at 10:00 stays at 10:00 and reminders move with
it. Anyone whose device is set to another zone sees a short note on the task and on
their booking. If everyone is in the same zone, nothing looks different.

![An event's settings with the time zone, its status and who can see it](./images/event-time-zone.png)

These now follow the event's clock too:

- **Messages:** If an event isn't in the usual time zone of the WirkSam site you use,
  messages name its zone after the time.
- **Calendars:** Shifts in your calendar carry their time zone, and a shift that runs
  past midnight ends the next morning.
- **Overlapping shifts:** If you book shifts in two events in different zones, the
  check for overlaps compares the real times.
- **Dates:** Whether an event is over, which day a venue map opens on and when a
  team's check expires all go by the event's date.
- **Buttons:** The buttons on shifts match what's allowed, even if you're far from the
  venue.

## Publish an event and choose who sees it

An event's settings now let you publish it and change who can see it. That includes
an event you copied, which starts as a draft.

## Pages about WirkSam, and short guides

The public site now has a page for each part of WirkSam, with screenshots and answers
to common questions. Short guides walk you through things like your first event,
your first shift as a volunteer or copying an event for next year.

## Messages

- Organisers get a push notification about half an hour before a shift that still has
  open places. E-mail for this can be switched on in the settings.
- If a shift you booked is removed, you get a message. Shifts that are already over
  send nothing.
- Someone added to a team directly gets a message.
- Organisers hear when a member accepts tasks through an invitation.
- A channel you switch off stays off, reminders included. Reminders still land in your
  inbox.
- The setting "Availability Reminder" is gone.

## Asking first

- Leaving an event, removing someone and handing an event over now ask first and say
  what will happen.
- Switching off approval for a team warns you when that would let people in who are
  still waiting.
- Expired invitations and share links are now marked as expired.

## Also new

- **Printing:** Organisers can now print the whole event, through the printer icon in
  the task list. Names can be left off a sheet, and its QR code leads to the event.
- **Places:** If you change a task's place, its shifts follow, unless a shift has a
  place of its own. Regenerating shifts keeps what you typed in by hand.
- **Members:** Members now see an event's details as plain text. Their settings have a
  new link to who's in the event, where they can also leave it.
- **Home screen:** Open requests lead straight to where you decide them.
- **Reports:** "Active Volunteers" now means people with a confirmed booking in the
  chosen period, and the total adds the members of the events in the report. The
  default period runs to the end of next month.
- **Vouchers:** An archived task no longer takes vouchers.
- **Venue map:** On a phone, "Use a picture" opens right where you choose the file.
- **New events:** A new or copied event opens with all its tabs, without reloading.
- **Wording:** Several explanations in the app now say more precisely what happens, and
  teams say "organisers" like the rest of the app.
- **Hosting WirkSam yourself:** `.env.example` leaves error reporting empty, so you
  only fill it in if you use Sentry.
