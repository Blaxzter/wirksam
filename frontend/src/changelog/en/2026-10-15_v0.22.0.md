## Put WirkSam on Your Home Screen

WirkSam can now be installed like an app. Open the menu behind your name and
choose **Install app**: on Android and in desktop browsers that brings up the
browser's own install prompt, and on an iPhone or iPad it shows you the two taps
through Share → Add to Home Screen. Installed, WirkSam opens in its own window
with its own icon, straight onto your dashboard. If the connection drops, you
get a clear "you're offline" page instead of the browser's error.

## Everything Within Reach of Your Thumb

On phones, the button in the top-left corner that opened the side menu is gone.
The bar at the bottom has a fourth tab, **More**, with everything that used to
hide behind it: Home, your availability, reports, the admin pages and What's
New. One hand is enough now. Tablets held exactly at the edge between phone and
desktop layout also no longer end up with no navigation at all.

## Tell Us What's Missing

The landing page ends with a new question: **Missing something?** From there
you can suggest a feature or report a bug. Both open a short form on GitHub; if
you don't have a GitHub account, an email works just as well. WirkSam is shaped
by the teams that use it, so if your event needs something it can't do yet, we
want to hear about it.
