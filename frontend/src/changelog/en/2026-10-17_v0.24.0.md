## Swipe Through a Job's Days

The days under each job on the jobs page are now a row you can swipe through.
It snaps to whole days, and as you near either end the next or earlier days
load on their own, as far as the job runs. On a phone a sliver of the next day
peeks in at the edge so you can see there is more. On a larger screen the
arrows are still there and move a screenful of days at a time. After you book
a shift the row stays where you left it.

## Finding Your Way on a Phone

On a phone, "More" in the bottom bar is now the one place for every page: home,
the map, your availability, switching events and, for organisers, reports and
managing events. The menu behind your picture at the top keeps what is about
you: your account, notifications, light or dark mode and signing out.

## Smaller Touches

- On the map, the plan picker sits in the free space at the top of the screen
  instead of behind the three dots, and the same in the editor.
- The map and the editor no longer run into the bottom bar.
- Reports fit a phone: the cards have room between them, and the breakdowns by
  category and by job are lists instead of tables you had to scroll sideways.
