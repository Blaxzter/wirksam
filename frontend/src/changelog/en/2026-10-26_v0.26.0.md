## Teams

Organisers can now group people by job or qualification, such as the cash
desk, the shuttle drivers or childcare, and reserve a task for a team. Only
the team's members can then book its shifts; everyone else sees a lock and can
ask to join right there. A team is either open to everyone or needs approval
from its leads or the event's organisers, who can also add people directly.

Teams have their own page in the sidebar, under Availability, with a badge
when someone is waiting for a decision. The task list, the dashboard, the shift
details and the venue map show which shifts a team holds, and the task list's
filter has a new "Only tasks I can book" switch, off by default. Events without
teams look exactly as before.

![The Teams page, with requests waiting for a decision](./images/teams-page.png)

## Checks Before You Approve

A team can list what people need before they join: an extended police
certificate, a first-aid course, a driving licence, or anything you write
yourself. When you review a request, you tick each check and, where asked,
enter the issue date; approval stays disabled until every check is ticked.
WirkSam keeps only that you checked it, when, and the issue date. When a check
runs out, the member is marked as needing a new one, without losing access.

![Reviewing a request with an attached certificate](./images/teams-review.png)

For some checks, like a first-aid course, people can attach their certificate
to the request. Only the team's leads and the organisers can open it, and it is
deleted as soon as the request is decided. A police certificate can never be
uploaded: you look at it in person.
