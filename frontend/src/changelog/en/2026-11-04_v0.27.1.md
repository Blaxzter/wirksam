## Messages only go to the people involved

When a task was published, every account on WirkSam got a message with its name,
even for private events and even if they had nothing to do with the event. Now only
the event's members are told.

## Reminders arrive on time

Reminders used to arrive late, by an hour in winter and two in summer. So "15 minutes
before" only came once the shift was already running. They now arrive at the time you
set, including for shifts you've already booked.
