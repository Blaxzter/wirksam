## A join request opens the request

When someone asks to join your event, the notification now takes you straight to the
People tab, where the request waits at the top. This works from the inbox, a push
notification and the email alike. An email about a team request now opens the team.

## Your event in the menu

If you run the selected event, it has its own entry under "Management". It folds out to
the event's tabs, from Details to Map, and a number shows how many join requests are
waiting. On a phone you'll find it under "More".
