## Sign-up lists without an account

Not everyone who helps once a year wants an account. Organisers, and people in charge of a task, can now share some of their shifts as a link: in the parents' group chat, in the club newsletter, or on a poster with a QR code. Whoever opens it picks a shift, types their name and is signed up, with no account and no app.

![A shared list on a phone: the shifts of two tasks, the places still free and the first names of those already signed up](./images/shared-list-public.png)

You'll find it under "Shared lists" in the sidebar, under "More" on a phone. "Share a list" takes three steps: which tasks and days, how the list looks, and then the link with a QR code and a text for your group. Out of the box, a list asks for an email address but not for a phone number, shows only how many places are free, lets one person take as many shifts as are free, and stays open until its last shift starts. You can change each of these per list.

![The first step of sharing a list: two tasks picked, and the days their shifts are on](./images/shared-list-wizard.png)

For organisers and people in charge, "Print" on a task's page now makes a notice with the QR code of such a list, so anyone passing the notice board can sign up without an account. If the task has no list yet, it asks once and creates one with the defaults, and "Adjust first" opens the steps instead. The notice lists the shifts still to come with their free places, and a line for each free place for those who'd rather write their name on paper. "Print all tasks" puts the whole event on one list, and "Print notice" in a list's details prints it too. Members still print the task's sheet, now without a QR code.

Helpers who give an address get a confirmation with a link to take their name off again, and can ask for a reminder by email the day before. Without an address, the page shows them that link to keep. On a tablet at the stand, "Done – next person" empties the form, and after two minutes without a touch it empties itself.

People who sign up without an account earn vouchers too, and redeem them with a code. The list shows how many vouchers each shift brings. Helpers show their code on the page behind their link, and the stand redeems it like a member's. Someone who signs up several times with the same address confirms it with "Yes, that's me" from the link in each confirmation email: the sign-ups confirmed for one event then show on one page, and their vouchers are added up.

![The Links tab with three lists, their status, the places still free and how many signed up](./images/shared-lists-page.png)

- The "Links" tab shows each list with its status and how many signed up. You can pause, resume or end a list or give it a new link, one at a time or several at once, and "Pause all" stops every list of the event in one go.
- The "Tasks" tab shows which free places are on no open list yet, and "What helpers see" shows the page as it looks right now.
- On rosters, in the hours and in the voucher overview, people who signed up through a list are marked "no account". Their email address and phone number go only to the organisers and the people in charge of the task.
- How long their details are kept is in the form, the confirmation email and on the page behind the link, there with the date. Vouchers not redeemed by then expire.
- Organisers can switch sharing off for people in charge.
- You can try lists in the demo event: as an organiser you'll find one there with three sign-ups already. A demo sends no mail, so whoever signs up sees the link to their sign-ups right on the page.

## Also new

- Organisers and people in charge can take people off a shift who signed up through a list: one at a time, a selection, or everyone who signed up after a given person, for example after a wave of made-up sign-ups.
- The page controls in user and event administration are labelled in your language and fit on a phone.
- When two people book the last place at the same moment, exactly one of them gets it, and a double tap books a shift only once.
- Booking a shift again after cancelling it no longer fails for people with default reminders.
- The report's CSV export has a last column with the list a booking came through.
- The privacy policy now covers signing up without an account.
- If you host WirkSam yourself: `SHARED_LISTS_ENABLED` switches the lists off, and `GUEST_DATA_RETENTION_DAYS` (30) sets how long the details stay after the last shift. The frontend container reads the same value for the privacy policy. Cloudflare Turnstile, if you set it up, protects sign-ups on lists as well, and while the lists are on, the backend warns at startup if it isn't set up.
